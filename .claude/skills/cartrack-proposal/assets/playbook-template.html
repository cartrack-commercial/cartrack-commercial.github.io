📜 Open "Before we talk price, let me show you exactly how you're insured today — then where I can put you in a stronger position." Then open the Cover Story.
The objections you'll hit — and the words that answer them
"Why move? I've been with them for years."
Loyalty's fair — but you're on two insurers, two renewals, two claims desks. This puts everything on one wording at a lower total, and the biggest item alone drops nearly Rx,000 a month.
"It's more expensive on two of my items."
It is — and I'll show you exactly why. The whole increase is Sasria; on the cover we're cheaper and you gain a bigger umbrella. A better position, not a worse one.
"What actually changes when I claim?"
The part you pay comes down — the everyday excess drops from R10,000 to R500. That difference lands in members' pockets the first time you claim.

🤝 Close like this "My honest recommendation: move everything onto one programme. Most save you money; the rest cost a little more and buy materially better cover. Shall I get it ready to bind while the quote's valid?"
🔧 The reframe that wins the room
Separate what's out of anyone's hands (Sasria, statutory, fees) from the cover the insurer actually underwrites. Nearly every "more expensive" line is the pass-through — say it plainly and turn an objection into proof you're on their side.
