# FAIS citation map

Source: **FSCA "RE 5 Task / QC", 14 January 2025, Version 1** — the regulator's published
qualifying criteria for RE5. Qualifying criteria originate in **BN 105 of 2008**, amended by
BN 151 of 2008, BN 64 of 2009, BN 95 of 2009, BN 44 of 2010, BN 60 of 2010, **BN 194 of 2017**
and **FAIS Notice 86 of 2018**.

Keyed by the topic you are writing about, not by exam task. Use it to find *which* provision
governs a point, then **read the provision itself** before citing it in anything signed. This map
is dated — verify against current law. Live copy: Drive → My Drive / RE5 →
`Re5 Task QC - 14 January 2025.pdf` (`193-G-eL-Wuhf4JV80reHBl0ZR7apqWDl`).

**Shorthand:** GCOC = General Code of Conduct for Authorised FSPs and Representatives
(BN 80 of 2003, as amended June 2020) · BN 194 = Fit and Proper Determination 2017 ·
FSR Act = Financial Sector Regulation Act 9 of 2017 · FICA = Financial Intelligence Centre Act.
