### Sasria divide-by-10 test (Western)
- Western prints **no annual Sasria figures** anywhere (summary p3 is monthly only), so the ÷10 test cannot be done off the face of the quote.
- **BI Sasria ties exactly at ÷12:** GP R12,100,000 x 0.10368% pa ÷ 12 = R1,045.44 (Western p3), the same statutory GP2 rate Natsure uses (R12,125,000 x 0.10368% ÷ 12 = R1,047.60, Natsure p44). Clean.
- **Material damage Sasria R3,612.19 does NOT fit ÷12.** Natsure's F2 rate is 0.047074% pa (R1,604.20 x 12 / R40,894,293.06, and identically R1,205.28 x 12 / R30,725,000). Western's insured property (buildings R40,583,500 + stock R34,325,864.47 + BAR R868,073 + EE R416,099 + contents R605,000 + glass R50,000 + AD R250,000 = R77.1m) gives **R3,024.38 at ÷12 and R3,629.26 at ÷10**. Printed R3,612.19 sits within 0.5% of the ÷10 result. Strong indication of the same ÷10 spreadsheet error seen on Lomaen, Vuyos and Lesedi, but not proof (rent, capital additions or theft may be in their Sasria base). If it is ÷10, true MD Sasria = R3,010.16, overcharge **R602.03/mo**, true total ≈ **R120,013.80**. **Ask FSP to print annual Sasria and its SI base per section.**
- Motor Sasria R1,141.29 and dealers R20.00 cannot be tested (no rate or base shown). Dealers R20.00 is double Natsure's R10.00 on the same R1,000,000 (R500k + R500k); may be a per-section minimum.
