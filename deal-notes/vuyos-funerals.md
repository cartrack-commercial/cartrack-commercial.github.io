## 🚨 SECOND FINDING — the current public liability excludes work away

The Old Mutual public liability section (R5,000,000, **claims made**, **retroactive date
01/09/2025**) carries two restrictions printed on the schedule:

> **Work away exclusion** — *"The company will not indemnify the insured in respect of liability
> arising from any work carried out elsewhere than at the premises occupied by the insured."*

> **Spread of fire exclusion** — *"…liability arising from any fire that has spread from the
> insured's premises."*

**A funeral is delivered away from the premises.** The service is at a church, the burial is at a
municipal cemetery, the family is at home, the body is carried in a hearse and a procession of
sixteen buses. **On the current wording, the public liability answers for almost none of it.**

- **Bryte writes work away INTO the limit** — *"General and Tenants/Workaway R5,000,000"*. A
  genuine upgrade over what the client holds, for R295.83 a month.
- **Santam and Auto & General are silent on it.** Both must confirm in writing whether liability
  away from the premises is covered. A&G's R158.47 for R5,000,000 is suspiciously cheap for a
  business with eleven addresses and daily off-site operations.

**This is the same shape as the Lomaen Medical finding** — a liability section that excludes the
thing the business actually does. It is the strongest cover argument in the file and it costs the
client nothing to fix.

⚠️ **Retroactive date 01/09/2025 is a help, not a hindrance.** Because the current cover only
reaches back twelve months, moving to a new claims-made policy with retro at inception forfeits
**one year** of tail, not decades. Still disclose it in the Record of Advice, but it is a small
price against closing the work-away hole.

## What nobody covers — including the policy they already hold

- **Deterioration of stock.** A funeral parlour **refrigerates bodies.** Not on the current policy,
  not on one of the three quotations. This is the characteristic loss of the trade — the same
  finding as CS Continental's cold rooms. ⚠️ And note the current Old Mutual schedule carries an
  **Electricity Grid Interruption exclusion** that names *"the deterioration of stock, food or other
  items"* as excluded consequential loss. **Load-shedding plus no deterioration cover is a live,
  named hole on the policy in force today.**
- **Business interruption.** R0 on the current policy, not selected on Auto & General, R0 on Bryte
  (its Sasria page prints BUS INT sum insured 0.00), not seen on Santam. Eleven premises and a
  R1.8m-a-year programme with no cover for lost income. **A standing gap, not a regression.**
- **Machinery breakdown** on the refrigeration plant — nowhere.
- Auto & General additionally has **money, fidelity, goods in transit, employers' liability and
  group personal accident** all not selected.
- ⚠️ **Santam's theft section covers ONE premises** — Devland — at R438.28. The current policy is
  R343.75 and Auto & General carries R125,000. **Bryte has no theft section at all.** Across eleven
  trading addresses that is a real gap on two of the three quotes.

**Credit where due:** Santam's business all risks section is properly written for the trade —
**undertaker's equipment R50,000 including two lowering devices**, plus medical, catering and
vacuum equipment. Bryte also schedules **2× lowering devices R50,000**. Auto & General specifies

⚠️ **An address is wrong on the current policy and has been copied into the quotes.**
