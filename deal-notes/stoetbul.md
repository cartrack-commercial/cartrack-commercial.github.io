## Gates before this goes to the client

1. **The quote is INDICATIVE.** Printed on page 1: *"acceptance of this risk and the rating applied
   are subject to a satisfactory three-year claims history and satisfactory loss ratio. Written
   confirmation from the previous insurer must be received prior to inception."* Get the three-year
   record from Efficient Insure and OUTsurance **before** the figure goes anywhere.
2. **All four vehicles have blank registration, VIN and engine numbers** — the schedule literally
   prints *"Please provide Registration Number!"* on each. Only the Hummer carries reg/VIN in an
   endorsement.
3. **Tracking.** Western requires an SABS-approved tracker on any vehicle over **R350,000** — the
   Jeep (R462,900), Everest (R1,004,500) and Ranger (R1,186,100), so **three of four**. All four
   items read **"TRACKING DEVICE: NONE"**. Penalty if not fitted: **additional excess of 20% of
   retail value** (Everest R200,900, Ranger R237,220); where two devices are required and not
   fitted, **no theft or hijack cover at all**.
   - OUTsurance records **Tracetec SVR** on the Everest and **Matrix Beam E-Plus** on the Ranger —
     so those two are covered, they just need declaring and confirming as acceptable to Western.
   - Santam shows the **Jeep with no alarm and no tracker**. That one is a real gap and a
     **Cartrack lead**.
4. **Business description mismatch — potential non-disclosure on the current policy.** Santam's
   schedule says **"Restaurant"**. Western says **"RESTAURANT, PUB, AND LIVE MUSIC VENUE"**. A pub
   with live music is a materially different risk. If the venue trades that way and Santam was only
   told "restaurant", the current policy carries a disclosure problem — which is an argument *for*
   moving, not against.
5. **Products liability drops R4.5m → R2.5m.** Ask Western to match R4.5m.
6. **Accidental damage costs R274.68 more** — confirm the sum insured matches Santam's R250,000.
7. **Not strictly apples for apples, in the client's favour:** Western adds **Houseowners R2,200,000
   on a main residence at 46 Mopani Road, Legacy Hill, Hennopspark** (R504.17) and **car hire
   R760.00** — neither is on the current programme. Strip those and the like-for-like saving is
   closer to **R6,198/mo**. Say it as a gain, not as a mismatch.
8. **Broker fee rises R1,103.31 → R1,978.24** (+R874.93). That is ours. Be upfront about it — the
   saving survives it comfortably.
9. **Car hire is only on the Hummer and Jeep** (R380 each); both Fords show R0.00. Probably an
   oversight on the two most expensive vehicles.
