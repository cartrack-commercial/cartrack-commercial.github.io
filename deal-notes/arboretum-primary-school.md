## Findings already visible, before a single premium

- ⛔ **The insured doesn't match.** The current policy names **three** parties — Arboretum Primary
  School, **Arboretum Pre-Primary School** and **FTRRI**. **The ONE quote names only "Arboretum
  Primary School".** A claim by an entity that is not a named insured does not pay. Same shape as
  the CGR Holdings finding on Integral Trading. **Fix before anything is presented.**
- ⚠️ **The current policy has NO theft section, NO business interruption and NO fidelity.**
  Confirmed from the surviving section summary. On a school that collects fees, **fidelity is the
  characteristic loss** — and there is none. Neither policy carries business interruption.
- ✅ **ONE adds Contents (R752.53)** where the current policy has Office Contents switched off.
- ⚠️ **The school runs buses** — Sasria M5 R463.45 and M8 R54.22 on the ONE quote. **Passenger
  liability has to be checked on both sides.** That is the line that decided Wes-Kaap Busdiens and
  is a standing gap at Vuyos Funerals. Nobody has raised it here yet.
- ⚠️ **ONE's dates are placeholders:** inception and endorsement read **01/01/3000** and payment
  reads **TBA**. **Identical to the Gear Lab ONE quote** — it appears to be how ONE issues an
  unaccepted quote, but it means **no start date is actually set**.
- ⚠️ **The renewal dates don't line up.** Current anniversary is **01/04/2027**; the ONE quote says
  renewal **01/08/2027**. Confirm which is intended, and time any cancellation so there is no gap
  and no double debit.
- ⚠️ **Marsh's own covering letter warns that average applies** to Buildings Combined, Office
  Contents, Fire and Electronic Equipment — and asks the client to confirm replacement values.
  **With R7,134.64/mo of electronic equipment on the ONE quote, the EE sum insured is the largest
  single line in the programme and must be verified on both sides.**

### ✅ The strength, and it is a real one

**The three buses each carry passenger liability of R5,000,000** under a bus-and-coach specific
extension, plus **third party liability of R5,000,000**. Fleet: 2013 Iveco 50C15V15 midibus
(CH98WYZN), 2008 Toyota Quantum 14-seater (CH07GJZN), 2018 Toyota Quantum 14-seater (CH98VTZN).

⚠️ **A near-miss worth recording.** The schedule prints *"Passenger Liability - Limited to
R2 500 000: No"* against every vehicle, which reads at first glance as passenger liability being
declined. It is not. The next line reads *"Passenger Liability R5 000 000 Limited to Buses and
Coaches: Yes"*. **The R2.5m standard option is off because the R5m bus version is on.** Read both
lines before reporting on this section.

**That is better than Wes-Kaap Busdiens and Vuyos Funerals both achieved**, and it is the line that
should be checked against the current Santam policy first.
