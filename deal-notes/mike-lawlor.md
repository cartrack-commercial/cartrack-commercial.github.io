## 🚨 THE FINDING — Santam is a trap as quoted

| | Current / Hollard | Santam |
|---|---|---|
| Main residence, Meyersdal | R30,360,000 | **R20,000,000** ⛔ |
| Danabaai | R6,140,000 | **R4,000,000** ⛔ |
| Vaal Marina | R2,380,000 | R2,380,000 |
| **Total buildings** | **R38,880,000** | **R26,380,000** |

**Santam is R12,500,000 short.** On a total loss of the main house the **average clause** applies
and it settles at roughly **66 cents in the rand** — about R10.36m of the client's own money.

Santam compounds it on the cars: **both collectors are written on Retail basis, not Agreed Value**,
and the **Dodge is mis-coded as a "1996 Base Coupe"** when it is a **1968 Charger**. A 1968 Charger
has no retail book value in any sensible sense; on that basis the R2,000,000 is not what would be
paid.

⚠️ **Santam's R992.09/mo saving is not a saving.** It is R12.5m of missing buildings cover and two
collector cars on the wrong settlement basis.

## The properties policy (TRQ / Tranquille)

**R11,763.02 → R11,564.35 = −R198.67/mo.** Reconciled.

⚠️ **The catch:** **Public liability moves from R50,000,000 on an occurrence basis to R20,000,000
on a claims-made basis.** That is two reductions in one line — the limit is cut by 60%, *and* the
basis changes so it only responds while the policy is live and the retroactive date reaches back
far enough. **For R198.67 a month this is not worth it as it stands.** Ask Tranquille to price
R50,000,000, and to state the retroactive date in writing.
