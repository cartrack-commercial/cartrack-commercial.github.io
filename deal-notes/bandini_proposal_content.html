So unless those claims all happened in one year, the reducers cost more than they would return. Tell us which years the claims cover and we will price the split with and without them.
Why tracking matters for you

R1,876,816 of your declared claims were theft. Under every quotation, a vehicle above the threshold without a working, monitored tracking unit has no theft cover at all. On your fleet that is roughly 90 to 110 vehicles. It should be in place whichever insurer you choose.

The law that matters most

Section 61 of the Consumer Protection Act makes the producer, importer, distributor and retailer each liable for harm caused by unsafe goods, without proof of negligence. Across the group you hold all four roles.

All five companies must be named on every policy. Every motor quotation names one company only.

What your new register tells us
A fourth site, "Potch"
The tractors, balers, grader, roller and older TLB are listed at "Potch". That site needs to be on your property and liability cover too. Please confirm the address.
R1,380,000 of forklifts
Eight forklifts, stackers and pallet jacks across the Impact, Bandini and Fresco Beverages sites. Bryte insures your special types for third party only, so damage to them is most likely not covered today.
Plant that uses public roads
Two registered Landini tractors, a roller, a TLB and the P&H crane. The plant policy in the split has no road third party cover, so these need it added or must stay on the motor policy.
A horse box and a game trailer
If you keep horses or game, animals in transit and livestock are a separate conversation.
Why we raise it in a fleet review South Africa's 2018 listeriosis outbreak showed how one contaminated product reaches every customer a distributor supplies. A food group's largest single loss is far more likely to come from a product than from a truck, and we have not yet seen any policy that shows this cover is in place.
