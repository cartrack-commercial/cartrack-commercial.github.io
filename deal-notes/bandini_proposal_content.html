What is included, and next steps

Trucks and trailers · King Price
  • Comprehensive on all 44 trucks and 31 trailers
  • Third party R2,500,000 (fire or explosion R1,000,000)
  • Contingent and unauthorised passenger liability R2,500,000
  • Towing R100,000 · wreckage removal R50,000
  • Fuel tank spillage R250,000 · loss of keys R50,000
  • Roadside assistance R20,000 a year per truck
  • Driver accident cover: R100,000 death or disability, R50,000 medical, two drivers per truck
  • Cover in seven neighbouring countries
Cars and bakkies · Old Mutual Insure
  • Comprehensive on all 43 vehicles
  • Third party R2,500,000 (fire or explosion R1,000,000)
  • Credit shortfall on financed vehicles
  • Car hire up to 30 days, R15,000
Tractors, forklifts and plant · Compass
  • Full all risks on all 40 items: damage, theft and fire
  • Plant liability R1,000,000
  • Credit shortfall, 20% value escalation
