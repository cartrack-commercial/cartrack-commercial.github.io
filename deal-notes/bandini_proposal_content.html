What this means for you On trucks the two are close, and Hollard is better on large accidents. On your 43 cars, SUVs and bakkies Hollard's excess is several times VAPS's, and small claims would not be paid at all. The claims you declared total R3,843,726, half of it theft, so we will ask both insurers to price their excess reducers before you compare the premiums.
Why tracking matters for you

R1,876,816 of your declared claims were theft. Under both quotations, a vehicle above the threshold without a working, monitored tracking unit has no theft cover at all. On your fleet that is roughly 90 to 110 vehicles. It should be in place whichever insurer you choose.

Three fleets, three counts

Bryte has 42 trucks and 30 trailers, VAPS 42 and 32, Hollard 40 and 47. The totals are close but the split is not. Your fleet list, vehicle by vehicle with agreed values, will settle it, and every insurer should quote on the same list.

The law that matters most

Section 61 of the Consumer Protection Act makes the producer, importer, distributor and retailer each liable for harm caused by unsafe goods, without proof of negligence. Across the group you hold all four roles.

All five companies must be named on every policy. Each of the three motor quotations names Impact Distributors only.
