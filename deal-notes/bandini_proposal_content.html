The cover that matters most for a food group

Products liability and recall

Section 61 of the Consumer Protection Act makes the producer, importer, distributor and retailer of goods each liable for harm the goods cause, whether or not anyone was negligent. Across the group you hold all four roles: Bandini Cheese and Fresco produce, Impact Distributors imports and distributes, and Elena's Delicatessen sells.

CoverWhat it pays forWhat to check on your policy
Products liability
Claims from people made ill or injured by your products, and the legal costs of defending them.Limit per event and in aggregate; all five companies named; imported products included; any "manufacturing or preparation" exclusion.
Product recall and contamination
The cost of withdrawing a batch from customers, destroying it, and the lost profit. Usually a separate policy.Whether you hold it at all; limit; whether government or customer-ordered recalls are included.
Public liability
Injury or damage to others at your sites or on delivery.Limit; spread of fire; work done at customers' premises.
Employers' liability

Dairy effluent and refrigerant leaks.Sudden and accidental only, or gradual too.
Retroactive date
if claims-made
A claims-made policy only answers for events after its retroactive date.The date must carry across to any new insurer, or past production is uninsured.
Why we raise it first South Africa's 2018 listeriosis outbreak, traced to a processed-meat factory, showed how a single contaminated product reaches every customer a distributor supplies. A food group's largest single loss is far more likely to come from a product than from a truck. We have not yet seen any policy that shows this cover is in place for your group.

Your claims, against a R30,000 minimum

The claims you declared total R3,843,726.04, of which R1,876,816 is theft. Under a standard 10% minimum R30,000 truck excess and 15% theft excess, those same claims would have cost you at least R496,893 yourself. Both insurers recognise this: Bryte by selling a buy-back, VAPS by offering excess reducers. We will put the two prices side by side.

Tracking is a condition of theft cover

VAPS pays no theft or hijack claim on a vehicle worth over R200,000 without an approved, working, monitored tracking unit with recovery: about 93 to 112 of your vehicles. Larger trucks on longer routes also need two-camera video telematics, and trailers their own trailer unit. With theft at half your claims, this should be in place whichever insurer you choose.
