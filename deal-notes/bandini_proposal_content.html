Not taken on this quotation
  • Goods in transit. The cheese on the trucks is not insured by this quotation, and nor is spoilage if a refrigeration unit fails on the road.
  • Excess reducers on own damage, theft and third party. Without them a truck claim starts at R30,000 (page 4).

  • Credit shortfall on financed vehicles.
  • Car and truck hire while a vehicle is off the road.
  • Riot and strike as an extension (Sasria is included and covers this).
The one that matters most You are a distributor. If a truck is hijacked, the vehicle is insured but the load is not. If your goods-in-transit cover sits on another policy today, we need to see it. If it does not, it should be priced before anything else is decided.

How a reducer works

You pay the standard excess and the reducer refunds everything above a small fixed "inner" amount. On the VAPS quotations we have placed this year, the reducers bring a truck or trailer excess down to R5,000 and a light vehicle to R2,500 or nil, sized so that even a total loss costs only that amount.

How we worked this out The claims figures are totals by type, not claim by claim, so we applied each percentage to the total and the R30,000 minimum to the trailer claim. Minimums per claim would raise the figure. Where a claim is later recovered from the other party, the excess is normally refunded, which lowers it. The 23h00 to 04h00 loading is not included; if your trucks leave before dawn it adds another 5% to those claims.

Five things to put right before cover starts

ItemWhat the quotation saysWhat has to happen
1. Tracking
a condition, not a discount
No theft or hijack claim is paid on a vehicle worth over R200,000 unless an approved tracking unit with recovery was fitted, working, paid up and monitored 24 hours a day. Heavy vehicles over R800,000 on medium and long haul need two-camera video telematics. Trailers over R200,000 need their own trailer tracking unit.Evidence vehicle by vehicle before inception. R1,876,816 of your claims were theft.
2. Agreed value
You asked for agreed values, but the quotation does not state agreed value anywhere. The wording then settles on retail value at the date of loss.Agreed value written onto the schedule, per vehicle, from your fleet list. It may not exceed retail plus 20%.
3. No vehicle list
The fleet is insured as nine groups with a count and a total. No registrations, no individual values. At each anniversary you declare the number of vehicles and the premium is adjusted.Your fleet list attached to the schedule, so every vehicle and its value is on record.
4. Your details
Name printed as "Impact Distributots"; business described as "Transport Contractor"; registration and VAT numbers blank; start date 1 October 2026, already past.Correct name, a description that says you distribute your own food products, company numbers, a new start date.
5. Claims record
The price is subject to three years of written claims from your current insurer, and may change if it does not match.Your current insurer's written claims history, so the price can be confirmed as firm.
Questions about how you operate
  • What time do the trucks leave? Driving between 23h00 and 04h00 adds 5% to the excess.
  • What are the 18 "special types"? Forklifts, refrigeration units or plant? It affects how they are rated.
  • Who rides in the bus? If it carries staff, passenger liability should be added.
  • Do you carry anyone else's goods for payment? If so, the business description must say so.
  • Where are the vehicles parked overnight, and are they under cover?
