Thank you for the quotation of 12 August 2026 on the above risk.

We have reconciled it line by line against the client's current Santam Heavy Haulage policy
(10000064816). The premium is attractive — R122,203.83 against R131,719.79 — but on the terms as
issued we are not able to put it in front of the client. The items below are what stand between the
quotation and a recommendation. Most are, we think, straightforward to correct.

For context: the insured is a **diesel haulage contractor** operating 27 units out of Wadeville,
with no forecourt, no retail site and no workshop.

## A · Must be corrected before we can present

**1 · Third-party liability is rated flat and needs to be rated by class.**
The quotation applies R2,500,000 to all 27 vehicles. The current policy rates by vehicle class:

- **R10,000,000** on the ten truck-tractors
- **R5,000,000** on the thirteen tankers and heavy trailers
- **R2,500,000** on the four light vehicles

Please re-quote on that basis. A loaded fuel tanker on a national road at R2.5m third-party is not a
limit we can recommend a client accept.

**2 · Goods in transit — the excess needs to come down.**
Both policies show a R1,000,000 load limit, so the sections look equivalent, but the excesses are
not. The current policy carries excess reducers bringing the first amount payable to a **flat
R20,000** for both own damage and theft/hijack. Yours is 10% of claim (minimum R1,500), with a
further 20% (minimum R2,000) on theft or hijack.

On a total loss of a R1,000,000 diesel load that is **R100,000, or R300,000 on a hijack, against
R20,000 today**. Please quote an excess reducer on the same basis.

**3 · Goods in transit — territorial limits and carry figure.**
The quotation states no territory. The current section covers the Republic of South Africa, Namibia,
Botswana, Lesotho, Eswatini, Zimbabwe, Malawi, Mozambique, Zambia, Tanzania, Angola and the
Democratic Republic of the Congo. Please confirm the territory you are offering.

The **Annual Carry Figure is blank** on your schedule. Please confirm what figure the section has
been rated on, so the client is not re-rated after inception.

**4 · The single-vehicle accident loading.**
Your motor section adds 10% of claim for a single-vehicle accident. The current policy has no such
loading. A rollover is the characteristic loss on a tanker fleet — this loading applies precisely
when the client is most likely to claim. Please remove it, or price the alternative.

**5 · Retroactive date on the liability section.**
Your quotation does not state one. The current public liability is written on a **claims-made basis
with a retroactive date of 20 August 2021**. Please confirm the retroactive date will be carried
across at 20/08/2021 or earlier. If it resets to inception, five years of the client's past work
loses cover, and we would have to disclose that to them in writing.

**6 · Basis of settlement.**
Not stated on your quotation. The current policy carries **Agreed Value on 23 of the 27 units** (with
a 10% agreed value variance), and retail value on the four light vehicles. Retail book value is not a
meaningful basis for a custom-built GRW, Afrit or Trailer & Tanker Technology tanker. Please confirm
Agreed Value on the specialist units.

**7 · A sum insured is understated.**
**MG45VLGP** (2025 Shacman X3000 480 RET) is shown at **R1,516,105** on your quotation and
**R1,816,105** on the current schedule — R300,000 short. Please correct it. Fleet totals are
R31,311,815 currently against R31,011,815 as quoted.

## B · Confirmations we need in writing

**8 · Has this been rated on the client's actual claims experience?**
Your quotation records "Loss History: **Tba**" and "Security details: **Tba**", and the footer
reserves the right to re-rate, to survey, and to levy additional premium for facultative
reinsurance. We are not willing to present a figure to a client that can move afterwards. Please
confirm the quotation is rated on the actual record, and advise any loading.

**9 · Public liability aggregate.**
You show R10,000,000 per event. The current policy is R10,000,000 per event and **unlimited in the
period of insurance**. Please confirm your aggregate.

**10 · Sasria.**
Yours is R11,858.00 against R9,883.62 currently — R1,974.38 higher. Sasria is a statutory
pass-through at identical rates, so the difference suggests a different exposure has been declared.
Please confirm the basis.

**11 · The schedule describes the wrong business.**
The quotation carries chilled refrigerated stock, petrol pumps and forecourt property, subsidence in
respect of fuel in underground tanks, till limits, petrol attendants, car-wash valet, forecourt
negligence and motor traders internal and external. This client has no forecourt and no retail site.
Please issue a schedule appropriate to a transport contractor, so that what is and is not covered
is clear at claims stage.

**12 · Broker fee.**
The broker fee line is blank. Please confirm.

## C · Please price these separately

**13 · Environmental impairment / pollution liability.**
Neither the current policy nor your quotation carries it — the current schedule reads
"Environmental impairment liability: Not included" against every vehicle. For an operator whose
entire business is the transport of diesel, a rollover contaminating soil or a water course is the
catastrophic exposure. Please quote it, on the motor section or standalone.

**14 · Roadside assistance.** Included on the current policy; yours lists it as available on request.

**15 · Credit shortfall.** Not carried on either policy. Investec Bank and Redtree Capital interests
are noted on the fleet, so please quote it for the financed units.
