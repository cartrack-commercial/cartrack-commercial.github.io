## ⛔ The four things Cules must raise before the client signs

### 1 · Retroactive date is inception — nothing already built is covered

The quote states **"Retroactive Date: Inception of this policy"**, on a claims-made basis. And the
CAR policy says in terms: **"RUN-ON COVER – Not included – Only new projects as and when policy
becomes effective."**

**Every installation Powerflow has already completed is uninsured for defective workmanship and for
design.** On solar PV and battery storage, latent defects surface years after handover — that is
the whole reason this cover exists.

**Ask AC&E to price a retroactive date.** If they will not offer one, the limitation must be
disclosed to the client **in writing** before they sign — it is a Record of Advice item, not a
footnote. This is the single most important sentence in the meeting.

### 2 · Defective Workmanship cannot be bought on its own — and the GPL is largely duplicate

Additional note (4) on the quote: *"Defective Workmanship is only applicable where the General
Public Liability option has been taken up."*

Powerflow already carries **R10,000,000 public liability on the CAR policy** (deductible R15,000),
plus R2m primary and a R20m excess layer on the commercial policy — or **R20m broadform ground-up**
if they move to Natsure as recommended. So the **R618.33/mo GPL is effectively the entry ticket to
the R1,391.33/mo Defective Workmanship section**, not new protection.

**Ask AC&E whether they will write Defective Workmanship over an external public liability policy.**
If yes, that is R7,420/yr saved. If no, say plainly that the GPL is the cost of admission — do not
present it as extra cover the client is gaining.

### 3 · The design / workmanship interlock — why one section alone leaves a gap

Defective Workmanship **specifically excludes** liability *"arising from defective design"*.
Professional Indemnity covers *"Design and Advisory electrical Installation Works and Solar"*.

- Bad **design** → Professional Indemnity
- Bad **installation** → Defective Workmanship

A real claim rarely arrives neatly labelled, and two insurers will point at each other. **Taking
one section and not the other leaves an arguable gap** on exactly the claim the client is buying
this for. That is the honest reason to take both — it is not a sales line, it is how the two
wordings interlock.

If budget forces a choice: **Powerflow designs and installs**, so PI alone (R2,473.42/mo) covers
the advice but leaves the workmanship, and GPL + DW alone (R2,009.66/mo) covers the workmanship but
leaves the design. Neither is comfortable.

### 4 · 🚨 The turnover declaration does not match the contracts

The CAR schedule declares:

- **Estimated annual turnover R15,000,000**
- **Maximum contract limit R6,000,000** — *"Should the Contract Value exceed this Maximum Contract
  limit, then such contract is excluded by this policy"*

And then carries, by endorsement:

> **Noted Contract — Decommissioning, Linden Lane — R22,000,000.00**
> (uninstall all solar panels and inverters on site and pack into a container; 273.24 kWp solar PV
> power system and 1001 kWh battery energy storage system). Start 13 Oct 2025. Excess **10% of
> claim min R35,000**. Added wef 10 Oct 2025.

**One noted contract is 3.7× the maximum contract limit and 1.5× the declared annual turnover.**
It is covered because it was specifically endorsed — which is the right mechanism, and credit to
whoever did it — but it tells you two things:

1. **Every contract above R6,000,000 must be individually noted or it falls outside the CAR policy.**
   Confirm nothing else is running unnoted right now.
2. **The R15,000,000 turnover figure is almost certainly wrong**, and **PI is rated on turnover**.
   If the real figure is materially higher, the AC&E premium is wrong and the policy is exposed to
   a non-disclosure argument at the first claim. **Get the actual annual turnover before signing.**

## Smaller items to close out before acceptance

- **The quote is incomplete.** Address reads *"Please Supply"*, e-mail and VAT number *"Please
  Provide"*. Both are on the CAR schedule: **VAT 4370276398**, **reg 2015/083526/07**. Fill them in —
  the quote's own conditions require the VAT and registration number on all closings.
- **Professional activities are marked "(PLEASE CONFIRM)"** — *"Design and Advisory electrical
  Installation Works and Solar"*. The Linden Lane contract involves a **1001 kWh battery energy
  storage system**. **Get battery energy storage named explicitly**; it is a distinct and
  fast-evolving risk and you do not want it argued at claims stage.
- **Defective Workmanship is R10,000,000 ANNUAL AGGREGATE**, where PI and GPL are each and every
  loss. Two bad jobs in one year share a single limit. Worth stating out loud.
- **Deductibles are steep.** PI R25,000 each and every loss; DW 10% of claim min R25,000 — a
  R500,000 workmanship claim carries a R50,000 first amount payable. Compare the CAR policy's
  R5,000 and its PL R15,000, so the client is not surprised.
- **Underground services condition.** Cover applies only if the insured inquired with the relevant
  authorities and service providers about position and depth **before** commencing works, and
  obtained wayleaves. Services **not** where the plans show them carry **10% min R25,000, or the
  schedule deductible, whichever is greater**; services **not on the drawings at all**, or damaged
  through failure to take due care, are **not covered**. For a contractor trenching cable this is
  an operating procedure, not a policy footnote — say it to the site manager, not just the director.
- **Defective Workmanship exclusions** beyond design: the cost of rectifying or recalling the work
  itself, inefficacy (it did not produce the result claimed), anything arising before handover, and
  aircraft work. **It pays for the damage the defect causes, not for redoing the job.**
- **Spread of fire** carries a condition precedent of compliance with the National Veld and Forest
  Fire Act, and excludes damage to sugar cane, crops, plantations and forests.
- **Exclusions to note:** war, terrorism, riot, strike, civil commotion, asbestos, computer losses,
  **gradual pollution**, and a worldwide **PFAS** exclusion. Environmental impairment is excluded —
  as it is on everything else in this client's programme.
- **Warranty:** no known claims or incidents between the proposal form date (**5 August 2026**) and
  acceptance, and the policy will not respond to anything known at acceptance. **Ask the client
  directly whether anything has arisen since 5 August.**
- **Cover incepts only when the premium is paid in full**, annually in advance unless the debit
  order authority is completed. **The quote expires 6 September 2026.**
- **Subject to a signed AC&E proposal form** and **confirmation of claims experience**, and AC&E may
  survey the risk.
- **Both existing policies sit with Pogir Bastion.** If the commercial policy moves to Natsure,
  decide deliberately whether the CAR policy moves too or stays — do not let it drift. Worth taking
  the CAR to market at its 01/03/2027 anniversary either way.
