The Naked Truth

DO NOT HAND
TO THE CLIENT

You are walking in to argue against two savings now — after the underwriter answered every question you put. A sharp client will test that. Here is where you are weak.

1
You are telling a business to spend R101,037 a year more — twice now.
Round one you said “let me go back to them”. They came back and fixed five of the fifteen. Hold again without naming exactly which two answers stopped you and it reads as stalling.
SAY"They answered everything. Two of the answers were no, and they're the two your own claims record says matter. Here they are."
2
Petrosure is genuinely better in three places.
Small accident excess min R5,000 against Santam's R30,000. Statutory legal defence R250k against R50k. Wrongful arrest R100k against R50k. If you pretend the quote is all bad, the client stops believing the rest.
SAY"There are three things it does better, and I'll ask for those to be kept in the re-quote."
3
The pollution gap is on the policy YOU are recommending they keep.
"Environmental impairment liability — not included" is on the Santam schedule, all 27 vehicles. If you present this as a Petrosure failing you will be corrected, and it will cost you the room.
SAY"This one isn't about the quote. It's missing on what you have now, and I want to fix it whichever way you go."
4
The quote is a filling-station template.
Chilled stock, petrol pumps, underground tanks, till limits, petrol attendants, car wash, motor traders. They have no forecourt. Useful to you — but do not sneer at it in front of the client; it makes the underwriter look sloppy and the client wonder about your market.
DORaise it with Petrosure in writing. In the room, just say the schedule needs to describe a transport contractor.
5
You know the claims record now — and Petrosure still has not rated on it.
R6,500,000, two trailers hijacked with their loads. W5101 still reads “Loss History: Tba” and is expressly subject to a satisfactory claims history. That price is going to move — and you can now prove it.
DOFile the record. MiWay priced with the R6.5m on the table and came in R5,795.73/mo ABOVE Petrosure. That is your evidence.
6
MiWay looks like the safe middle option. It is not.
It quotes 20 units against 27 — R4,838,555 unpriced: all four light vehicles, both Paramount trailers and the R2,415,000 tanker awaiting registration. And LW22DGGP is on the quote at R1,515,000 against your R2,400,000 — R885,000 short, so average applies. It carries no public liability section at all against your R10m with an unlimited aggregate, and its motor is R2,612.69 dearer than Santam for those seven fewer vehicles.
SAY"It's cheaper because it's smaller. And one of your tankers is on it for nine hundred thousand less than it's worth."
7
MiWay's paperwork describes a different business.
Ten vehicles in the last three years (they run 27). Previous insurer “undisclosed”. Risk address No 1 Melrose Blvd, not Wadeville. Day and night parking both “VARIABLE” — after two hijackings. And the losses are logged as “vehicle accidents”, both December 2025, not two hijackings a year apart.
DOCorrect it in writing before anyone treats R129,095.75 as firm. A misdescribed risk is a declined claim.

The objections you'll hit — and the words that answer them
"Eight thousand a month is eight thousand a month."
It is. It's also six percent. What you'd hand over for it is half the third party cover on every horse in the yard — and that's their ceiling, not their opening offer — plus a hijack excess that goes from twenty thousand to three hundred thousand. You've had two hijacks in two years. One more costs you thirty-three months of that saving.
"Profile Risk have looked after us for years."
I'm sure they have. I'd just ask you to look at one line on your own schedule — environmental impairment liability, not included, on all twenty-seven vehicles. You move diesel. That's the cover that answers when a tanker goes over and it reaches a river.
"So you're telling me to do nothing?"
No — I've already had five things fixed on that quote, including a vehicle that was three hundred thousand under-insured. I'm placing your pollution cover through a specialist market this week and I want credit shortfall on the financed units. What I'm not going to do is move you onto a thinner hijack excess to win your business.
"What if Petrosure won't budge?"
On third party they reach five million and no further — a hard ceiling, in writing. On the hijack excess they have already said no. So if neither moves you stay at Santam, and I take the whole account to the market properly before your next anniversary with your claims record in hand instead of “TBA”.
"MiWay is a big name and it's cheaper. Why not that one?"
Because it's cheaper for a reason I can point at. It quotes twenty of your twenty-seven vehicles — both new FAW trucks and the two-point-four million trailer aren't on it — and it removes your public liability section completely. You carry ten million per event with no annual cap today; on their quote that line doesn't exist. Their motor section is actually dearer than what you pay now, for seven fewer vehicles.
"You keep saying the price will change. Prove it."
MiWay is the only one that priced you with the six and a half million on the table — it's printed on their quote. They came in nearly six thousand a month above Petrosure, and Petrosure's document still says loss history to be advised. That's not a hunch, that's two quotes side by side.
