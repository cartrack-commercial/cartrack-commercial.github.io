## 🚨 Tracking — the current policy has a live theft-cover hole, and it is the Cartrack lead

ONE endorsement **HCV0019** and the LDV memorandum make theft cover **subject to** an approved,
paid-up, 24h-monitored tracking-and-recovery device on any self-propelled vehicle over
**R500,000** — that is **20 units** (15 HCV, 2 LDV, 3 Henred trailers at R970,000).
**All 23 items on the schedule read "Tracking device Required: No"** and no device is named
anywhere. The Feb 2025 theft of **R581,842** was paid; the next one need not be.

VAPS is stricter and spells out the whole build (effective 1 Mar 2023):

| Requirement | Units on this fleet |
|---|---|
| Tracking **with recovery**, value > R200,000 | **43** (18 HCV, 2 LDV, 23 trailers) |
| **2-camera video telematics + tracking + monthly driver monitoring**, long-haul HCV > R800,000 | **14 HCV** |
| Non-wired trailer tracker, battery ≥ 2 yrs, one per combination | 23 trailers |
| **GIT load limit ≥ R1.2m → independent units on BOTH trailer links, wired to Suzi power, 5-day battery backup, trailer-disconnect alerts** | all 18 combinations |
| VESA 3/4 or factory alarm/immobiliser, value < R200,000 | 4 |

MiWay is the same in substance: *"no theft and hijack cover if the vehicle does not have a MiWay
approved stolen vehicle recovery device with early warning functionality"* — but it names
Tracker and Netstar and then adds "or any SAIAS or VESA approved" (**get Cartrack named in
writing**), while separately listing "MiWay Required Tracking Device: **EXCLUDED**" on the fleet.
That contradiction has to be resolved before anyone relies on the quote.

**43 units + 14 dual-camera video telematics + driver scoring.** With 43 accident losses in three
years the telematics is not an upsell, it is the fix for the loss ratio that is pricing this risk.

## Recommendation — OWNsurance / Renasa, conditionally

The only quote that is **cheaper and holds the decisive cover**: third-party liability stays at
**R10,000,000**, agreed value is kept, GIT stays at R2,000,000 per vehicle with driver fidelity,
and the excess structure is transformed — **no excess at all on own damage, theft, hijack or
third party** under OwnRship Benefit Option A, against a flat R10,000 today, and **R5,000 on GIT
against R50,000 today**. Add side-tank clean-up R150,000, 18 non-accidental tows, standing-time
cover at R2,250/day to R45,000, GAP between agreed and market value, and Sasria bridge cover.

### ⚠️ The fund — do not repeat the Waste Carriers mistake

The quote's **"Annual savings R341,670.46"** and **"Annual savings GIT R163,800.00"** are **NOT
savings against ONE.** They are the client's own money going into the client's own fund, out of
which the client's own first claims are paid. It reconciles exactly, with **sequential** commission
deduction:

- Motor: R59,597.15 × (1 − 9% binder) × (1 − 12.5% broker) × **60%** × 12 = **R341,670.46**
- GIT: R37,500.00 × (1 − 9% binder) × (1 − 20% broker) × **50%** × 12 = **R163,800.00**
- Total fund **R505,470.46/yr = R42,122.54/mo**. Cartrack remuneration ≈ **20.375%** of motor
  premium and **27.2%** of GIT premium.

**Effective annual cost by claims scenario — the honest way to show it:**

| Scenario | Effective annual cost |
|---|---|
| No claims (full fund returned) | **R1,099,102.70** |
| R250,000 of claims | R1,349,102.70 |
| Fund fully consumed | **R1,604,573.16** |
| **Stay at ONE (gross)** | **R1,780,451.28** |

Even with the fund entirely spent, OWNsurance is **R175,878/yr cheaper**. That is the number to
lead with, because on this claims record the fund *will* be spent: R1.2m/yr of losses against a
R505,470/yr fund exhausts it in roughly five months. **Sell it as a zero-excess policy, not as a
savings plan** — Option A carries the zero excess through even after depletion, which is exactly
what a fleet with 43 accident losses in three years needs. Promise the rebate and you will be
explaining yourself at renewal.

### Gates before binding OWNsurance

1. **Written confirmation it is rated on the actual loss record** (R2,402,455 / 24 months).
2. **Territory** — GIT covers 11 countries; **Angola, Uganda, Rwanda and Kenya drop off.** Ask for
   them back or get the client's written acknowledgement.
3. **What do the tankers carry?** Third-party liability is R10m for general HCV but
   **R5,000,000 for petrol/diesel** and **R2,500,000 for chemicals**. The fleet has fuel trailers,
   DC tankers and a tri-tank. If any petroleum moves, the headline R10m is not what applies.
4. **12-month lock-in.** Cancel inside the year and **any surplus in the fund is forfeited**; a
   negative balance is payable on demand. Disclose this in writing.
5. **Values adjust only at renewal** (12 months) — no mid-term SI corrections.
6. **Environmental contamination and pollution liability excluded** on GIT. Place EIL separately.
7. **Overloading**: max 5% allowed; penalty excess = overload % × 2, not covered by the fund.
8. **Confidentiality clause** bars the client from disclosing the product structure to any other
   broker or underwriter. Flag it — it is a lock-in the client should see before signing.
9. OWNsurance quotes **no public liability**. Current carries **R20,000,000**. Either keep the ONE
   liability section standing or place it — do not let it fall away silently.

## Why the other three are benched

**VAPS / King Price — dearer AND materially thinner.** +R605.84/mo, and it cuts third-party
liability from **R10m to R2.5m (R1m if fire or explosion)**, public liability R20m → R10m,
parking/moving third-party vehicles R5m → R1m, cross-border towing R30,000 → R20,000, and the
territory from 16 countries to 8. The **capsizing-whilst-tipping loading becomes 5% of the
value of the vehicle** (R72,695 on a R1.45m MAN) where ONE charges 5% of claim — and there are
four tippers on this fleet. The GIT theft excess is **30% of claim on groceries**, and the
reducer caps at R500,000, so a R2m groceries hijack costs the client **R100,000**, not R50,000.
Genuine wins — nil third-party excess, loss of keys R50,000, towing R100,000, fuel-tank spillage
R250,000, driver assist at R100,000 death / R50,000 medical for two per truck, and a **R0 broker
fee** — do not buy back R7.5m of third-party liability. **No.**

**Natsure / Compass — dearest, and the GIT territory collapses to RSA only.** +R2,131.93/mo with
GIT at R54,000 against R28,800 (+87%). It is the **only** quote carrying **passenger liability
R2,500,000**, broadform liability is **R10m per address** rather than collectively, and the GIT
excess was negotiated down to a flat R50,000 matching ONE exactly — good work by the underwriter.
But **goods are covered in the Republic of South Africa and nowhere else**, and third-party
liability halves to R5m. Two further errors to raise: the HCV sum insured is **R16,584,900 against
R17,462,400 on every other quote — KD45LTGP (2017 MAN, on retail value at ONE) appears to carry no
value at all**; and the commodity is written as **"Groceries"** while the fleet runs tankers and
tippers. Re-quote only if they extend the GIT territory and correct the SI. **Not as quoted.**

**MiWay — cheapest headline, not a comparable policy.** −R10,964.67/mo without driver dishonesty,
but: every vehicle moves to **retail value** where 17 of 18 HCVs are on **agreed value** today
(fatal for 2010–2018 MANs); GIT becomes a **single R2,000,000 load limit** instead of R2m per
vehicle across R36m; the **GIT excess doubles to R100,000**; **driver dishonesty is EXCLUDED on
GIT in both versions** while ONE includes driver fidelity; **"Cover in Neighbouring Countries" and
"Cover in Extended Territories" are EXCLUDED on both the motor and the GIT sections** — RSA only;
no passenger liability, no passenger accident, no credit shortfall, no loss of fuel, no
environmental transport liability; third party R5m; emergency medical **R750 per passenger**
against R15,000 today. Trailers cost **R34,772.65** against R13,330.07 at ONE — three times the
price for the same 25 trailers. And there is a **R13,289.44/mo broker fee** on top, which is ours
and would have to be justified. The R8,777.56/mo difference between the two MiWay versions is
**driver dishonesty on the motor section only** — it never reaches the GIT section either way.
**No.**
