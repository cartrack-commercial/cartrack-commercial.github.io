Why Santam and not Hollard, when Hollard looks cheaper Hollard covers only the drivers named on the schedule, gives R1,000,000 of third-party cover per vehicle instead of R2,500,000, covers no passengers, and provides no theft or hijack cover on 24 of your 25 vehicles until a tracker certificate is on file. For a guarding company with rotating drivers that policy does not work. Page 04 has the detail.

Your current policy does not cover the people in your vehicles Every one of the 25 items on the Bryte schedule reads "Additional Passenger Liability: No", and the armoured Land Cruiser used at the mine carries a note that reads "Passenger Liability is excluded." If a guard is injured in one of your bakkies on the way to a post, the current policy has nothing for it. Santam includes R2,500,000 on every vehicle inside the premium. That is the single biggest reason to move.
Public liability: the limit is low everywhere, and Santam's has to be fixed before you sign All three policies carry R1,000,000. For guards working on a mine site that is thin. Santam's quote goes further and states "Public Liability EXCLUDES WORKAWAY", which would exclude the client's premises, the only place you have liability. We are having work away written in and R5,000,000 and R10,000,000 priced before this is presented as final. Hollard, for its part, drops wrongful arrest and legal defence, which a security company needs.
And the excess Bryte: 10% of the claim, minimum R10,000 per vehicle. Santam: 5%, minimum R4,000. On a R60,000 repair you pay R4,000 instead of R10,000. Across 25 patrol vehicles that is worth more than the premium difference.

What we fix before you sign, and what nobody has quoted yet

Corrections and open items

CORDIGUARD
On the Santam quote, our side
  • Work away written into the public liability, and R5,000,000 and R10,000,000 priced.
  • Ranger JN04CFGP to comprehensive. It was quoted on a limited basis by mistake.
  • Accidental damage back to R360,000 over both sites. Santam quoted R150,000 at Trezona only and left out the mine site.
  • The Samsung Galaxy S24 back on at R21,000 (it is quoted at R0), unless you no longer have it.
  • Interests noted: ABSA, Wesbank, Ford Credit, Phezulu Oil, and Multi Source Distribution Agency as owner of the two Vitz. Santam currently shows none of them.
  • Third-party liability to R5,000,000 on every vehicle, the figure your current schedule shows.
  • Start date moved to 1 October 2026 so there is no gap and no double debit. The quote is valid to 23 September.
From you
  • Registration numbers for the three JAC bakkies, the BMW X6 M and the two Vitz. Every schedule has them blank.
  • The BMW's year: Santam says 2023, Bryte says 2022.
  • Your claims over the last three years. Santam's price is conditional on confirming them. We know of the settled repair on Ranger KV57DMGP.
  • Where each vehicle sleeps. The Land Cruiser is at the mine; the rest are recorded at Trezona Avenue.
Trackers, on every policy Your current policy already requires a tracking device on 16 of the 25 vehicles (all the Rangers, all the Polos, everything over R750,000, two on the Fortuner) and none is recorded. Santam requires them on four and adds a 20% excess if security is not as declared. Hollard gives no theft cover at all without one. Cartrack units on all 25 settle this on whichever insurer you choose, and give you patrol-route proof for your own clients.
Not on any of the three policies, and worth a conversation Group personal accident for your guards (assault or injury on duty, over and above COIDA) · fidelity (a client's loss caused by one of your own guards) · employers' liability · business interruption. None of these was ever quoted by any of the three insurers. Group personal accident and fidelity are the two we would price first for a guarding company, and many of your own clients' contracts ask for them.
