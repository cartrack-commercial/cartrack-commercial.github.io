What we will take up for you

Six points from reading the three policies

PointWhat we will do
1. Fleet cancellation
Ask Bryte in writing for the cancellation notice and the due date it applied, and for reinstatement or a refund of the R741,038.66 paid on 20 and 21 October. Place replacement cover as a priority.
2. Fleet list
Your register shows 109 trucks and 238 trailers; the schedule of 1 August carried 110 trucks and 205 trailers. Several trucks are being returned or handed in. The replacement will be placed on your current list, with values.
3. Buildings escalation
On the Bryte quotation the escalation lines are rated at 9% and 4.5% instead of 0.09% and 0.045%, which makes them 100 times too high (R66,231 a month), and they are left out of the total. Two escalation amounts are also wrong. We will confirm with Bryte that escalation cover is in force and correctly charged (about R662 a month).
4. Buildings insured name
The quotation names Hendrik van Wyk Vervoer & Purple Rain, not Routelogic. We will confirm that every company that owns a building or carries the business is named.
5. Goods in transit revenue
Section B is rated on declared haulage revenue of R5,495,600,000 a year. The deposit premium adjusts upwards only, so an overstated figure is paid for. We will confirm the figure with you before the next declaration. The printed rates also do not reproduce the premiums; we will ask Navigate to explain.
6. Liability
Public liability is R1,000,000 under a R20,000,000 umbrella. We will check the umbrella's minimum underlying limit and whether R1 million is enough for your premises and yard.
What we need from you to place the fleet The fleet register with a value for every unit and who holds title (finance houses and lessors are noted on the policy) · a three-year claims record from Bryte and Lombaards · confirmation of which units are comprehensive and which third party, fire and theft · the tracking unit on each truck and trailer · licence renewals: 18 trucks and 4 trailers in your register show licence discs past their expiry date, and an unlicensed vehicle can prejudice a claim.
