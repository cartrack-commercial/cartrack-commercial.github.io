Infectious Or Contagious Disease Exclusion
Notwithstanding any condition, provision, extension or endorsement to the contrary, this policy does not cover any loss or damage:
1. Directly or indirectly caused by, happening through, in consequence of or contributed to by an Infectious or Contagious Disease;
2. Arising from any fear or threat ( whether actual or perceived ) of such Infectious or Contagious Disease;
3. Directly or indirectly caused by, resulting from or in 
connection with any action taken in controlling, 
preventing, suppressing or in any way relating to any 
outbreak of such Infectious or Contagious Disease.
Infectious or Contagious Disease, which shall include but is not limited to:
a. Human Immune Virus (H.I.V), Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS) or an AIDS related condition;
b. Any Influenza A Viruses or any mutant variation thereof; including but not limited to:
i)  H1N1, H3N2, H5N1;
ii) Avian Influenza, Bird Flu
iii) Any virus and/or conditions derived from the foregoing;
c. Any Coronavirus (CoV) or any mutant variation thereof, including but not limited to:
i) Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome (SARS-CoV);
ii) Middle East Respiratory Syndrome (MERS CoV);
iii) SARS-CoV2 or Covid-19;
d. Ebola Virus Disease (EVD) or any mutant variation thereof;
e. Any virus that can that can lead to pandemic or epidemic diseases listed by the World Health Organisation;
f. An outbreak of which a competent authority has stipulated shall be notified to them.
If the Insurers allege that by reason of this exclusion, 
any claim is not covered by this insurance the burden 
of proving the contrary shall be upon the Insured.
