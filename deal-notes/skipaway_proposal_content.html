Recommendation Price is not a reason to move, and it is not a reason to stay. VAPS gives you better roadside assistance, windscreen cover and excess reducers for the same money on the ten vehicles you already insure. Reasons to move
  • Roadside that works for a truck: R20,000 a year each on the Fuso and the tanker, towing up to 250 km, accident management and no storage or release fees.
  • Excess bought down to R5,000 on the trucks and trailer, R2,500 or nil on the light vehicles, even on a total loss.

The decisive cover · your 8,000 litre diesel tanker

Insured as a truck, not as a tanker

On a vehicle that carries 8,000 litres of diesel, the losses that matter are a fire and a spill of the load. As quoted, the tanker is insured for accident damage and theft like any other truck, and neither of those two losses is properly covered.

The spill clause, in the quotation's own words The fuel tank spillage extension pays up to R250,000 to clean up a leak "from an insured vehicle's fuel tank or engine", and then says: "This extension does not provide cover for any costs and expenses associated with the spillage of Dangerous Goods as identified and classified by the code of practice SABS 0228." Diesel is a Class 3 dangerous good under that code. If the tanker overturns and loses its load, the clean-up is for your account.
Is the tanker insured today?

It is not on your Santam policy. If it is already on the road it is either insured somewhere else, in which case we need that schedule, or it is not insured at all, in which case it should not wait for this decision. Please tell us which.

What we will ask VAPS for
  • Envirosure HAZ pollution liability on the tanker, priced
  • Increased third party liability, including fire and explosion, priced
  • The tanker declared as a dangerous goods carrier
  • Model year, finance house and registered owner completed
Our position We will not recommend any policy that insures the tanker without cover for a fire and a spill. Both additions will raise the VAPS price, and you will have that figure in writing before you are asked to decide.
What the insurer will ask when the tanker is declared
What does it carry, and for whom?
Your own diesel for the abattoir and fleet is a different risk from delivering fuel to customers. The answer decides how it is rated.
Who drives it?
A driver carrying dangerous goods needs a professional driving permit endorsed for dangerous goods. Please confirm each driver holds one.
Is it marked and documented?
Placards and the emergency information for the load, carried in the cab.
Where does it stand overnight and where is it filled?
The tanker is the one vehicle whose overnight location matters to the neighbours as well as to you.

The honest ledger · what you gain, and what needs correcting

Nothing here is kept from you

What the VAPS policy includes
  • Roadside and accident assistance, R20,000 a year on each truck and R5,000 on each light vehicle, with mechanical breakdown towing up to 250 km.
  • Windscreen cover of R15,000 on each vehicle except the trailer.
  • Contingent liability R2,500,000, when someone uses their own vehicle on your business.
  • Unauthorised passenger liability R2,500,000.
  • Parking facilities and movement of third party vehicles R1,000,000.
  • Towing and salvage R100,000, wreckage removal R50,000, cross-border towing R20,000.
  • Loss of keys R50,000, temporary repairs and fire extinguishing charges R35,000 each.
  • Cover in seven neighbouring countries: Namibia, Eswatini, Botswana, Lesotho, Zimbabwe, Mozambique and Malawi.
What must be corrected before inception
  • Your business is described as "Transporter". Santam records it as an abattoir with cold storage and a hire-out service. An insurer can decline a claim on a risk that was described wrongly.
  • If any vehicle is hired out to someone else, VAPS must be told. Santam records "hire out service" on the trailer.
  • The Hyundai's registration is printed as MVBITNGP. It is MV81TNGP.
  • Gran Max DJ6WDGP is a 2014 on Santam and a 2019 on VAPS, at the same value. Please check the licence disc.
  • Claims preparation costs are limited to R5,000 on VAPS.
The condition that matters most VAPS will not pay a theft or hijack claim on any vehicle worth more than R200,000 unless it had an approved tracking unit with a recovery service fitted and working, on a paid subscription, monitored 24 hours a day by a manned control room. That is four vehicles: the tanker, the Fuso, the Hyundai and the Hilux. Vehicles under R200,000 need a VESA level 3 or 4 or factory-fitted alarm or immobiliser. The quotation records nothing in its tracking fields, so this has to be evidenced vehicle by vehicle before cover starts.
