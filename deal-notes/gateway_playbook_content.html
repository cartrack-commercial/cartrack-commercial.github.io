Finding one, against King Price Passenger liability is printed "Private and LDV only" on all 25 vehicle items. Gateway runs 21 Toyota Quantum Sesfikile 15 and 16 seaters. Finding two, for King Price It is the only quote that has been rated as a school. Business description "Schools / swimming schools", R25,000,000 public liability, teacher liability, pupil liability including pupil-to-pupil, and R1,000,000 professional indemnity. Bryte's liability section reads "Industry: Unspecified". Auto & General carries no schools wording at all and R5,000,000 of public liability.
So the play is Go back to King Price on the passenger liability before any number reaches the school. If it comes back fixed, King Price is a clear recommendation at R1,542 a month above Bryte. If it refuses, the conversation changes to Bryte re-rated as a school, and you will need a second pack.

Play 02 · the gate

Second time this insurer has printed a limit that does not fit the vehicle

Play 02

On every one of the 25 King Price vehicle items the schedule reads, word for word: "Passenger liability: Private and LDV only R3,000,000" and "Unauthorised passengers liability: Private and LDV only R3,000,000".

Why this matters more here than anywhere else Gateway is a school for special-needs children and the buses are the business. Twenty-one Quantum Sesfikile 15 and 16 seaters run those children to school and home again every day. Passenger liability is not a secondary section on this account. It is the cover that answers if a bus full of special-needs pupils is in an accident.
We have seen this exact trap before, from this insurer Wes-Kaap Busdiens. King Price via VAPS quoted a scheduled bus operator and printed a passenger liability limit on an e-hailing RTU form, which would not have answered for commuter, scholar and charter work. We caught it on page 10 of the schedule, per item rather than per summary, and it became the gate on that deal. Same insurer, same shape, different wording. Keep this precedent internal. Put it to King Price as a question about this schedule, not as an accusation about the last one.

Ask for exactly this, in writing "Please confirm that passenger liability and unauthorised passenger liability under COM58558 respond in respect of Toyota Quantum Sesfikile 15 and 16 seater minibuses used for scholar transport, or reissue the schedule with the 'Private and LDV only' restriction removed and the limit stated per vehicle." Do not accept a verbal yes. The restriction is printed on the schedule 25 times, so only a reissued schedule or an underwriter's letter undoes it.
If the answer is no Then the recommendation flips to Bryte, re-rated as a school, and you tell the client the truth: the quote with the best liability section for a school could not cover the children on the buses, so we went the other way. That is a story that builds trust. Presenting the R3,000,000 and having it fail at a claim is the story that ends the relationship.

Four admin gates before anything is presented 1. The VAT number on the Bryte quote is 9517532. Seven digits. A South African VAT number is ten and starts with a 4. Same error shape as the Gear Lab quote. Get the real one.

Play 05 · the unit lead

Twenty-four of the twenty-five buses have no tracker, and both insurers want one

Play 05

This is the strongest telematics lead in the book right now, because it is not a suggestion. It is written into the quotes as a condition of theft cover.

What the Bryte schedule actually says Under Tracking Device, on 21 of the 25 items, the entry is "Early warning system required". Three more read "Unspecified". Exactly one vehicle shows a tracker fitted, and it is a Netstar unit. Security on the rest is "Factory fitted immobilizer" and nothing more. So on Bryte's own paperwork, 24 of 25 vehicles do not meet the condition the schedule prints against them.
And what King Price says, with a price on it "It's hereby declared and agreed that the security requirements for theft cover are: all vehicles must be equipped with an immobilizer or a tracking system. Vehicles with a value of R500,000 or more must be equipped with a tracking system. It's warranted that the tracking system is properly maintained and tested at least every 6 months and after every service."

"Should the insured not comply with the security measures applicable to the motor section, an additional excess of 25% of the claimed amount will apply."
And Auto & General pays you to fit one Its theft excess is R5,600 or 7.5% of the value of the vehicle, whichever is higher, and the schedule then says the client does not pay that excess at all if a tracking device is fitted. On a R450,000 Quantum that is R33,750 of excess wiped out by a unit. You do not need to sell the insurance benefit. Read the sentence off their own quote.
Two more angles on the same fleet Scholar transport with special-needs children. Live location and driver behaviour scoring is the thing a governing body understands immediately, and it is the thing a parent asks about. Lead with the child, not the excess.

Daytime parking on the Auto & General schedule reads "ROAMING", night parking "in yard with locked gates". Twenty-one buses roaming all day with no tracking on twenty-four of them is the risk in one line.
Size of the lead 24 units to fit, one Netstar unit to convert, and one competitor account to take off Netstar. Whichever insurer wins the policy, the tracking condition stays. Quote the units before the policy incepts, so the client is compliant on day one rather than finding out at a claim.

Play 06 · in the room

Objections, and how to close

Play 06
"Why are you recommending the one that is not the cheapest?"
"Because fifteen hundred rand a month is not a rating difference here, it is a different policy. King Price has quoted you as a school. It gives you twenty five million of liability, teacher liability, pupil liability including one child injuring another, and a million of professional indemnity that covers wrongful expulsion of a scholar. The Bryte quote has your industry recorded as unspecified, so none of that is on it. On a school for special-needs children, that is the section that decides it."
"So King Price it is then."
"Not yet, and I want to be straight with you about why. On all twenty five vehicle items their schedule restricts passenger liability to private cars and light delivery vehicles. Your twenty one Quantums are minibuses. As it is written today, the three million printed next to each bus would not answer. I have gone back to them in writing. I am not putting a price in front of you until that comes back corrected."
"What about Auto and General?"
"It is the dearest of the three and it covers the least. Five million of public liability against twenty five, no employers liability, no fidelity, no glass, and accidental damage at twenty five thousand against seventy five. It holds passenger liability properly, which the others do not all do, but that is the only place it wins and it costs four to five thousand a month more."
"How does this compare with what we pay Alpha now?"
"I cannot tell you yet and I am not going to guess. We do not have your current schedule. Send me the Alpha ALPHACOM204975 schedule and I will have that answer in a day. What this pack does tell you is which of the three is the right policy for a school, and that holds whatever Alpha is charging."
"We have never had a problem, why change anything?"
"Two things I would want fixed whoever you stay with. There is no employers liability on any of these three, and you have teaching and support staff. And twenty four of your twenty five buses do not have a tracking device, while the schedules in front of me make theft cover conditional on having one. One insurer charges an extra twenty five percent of the claim if it is not there."
Close like this "My recommendation is King Price, and it is conditional. I have one item with their underwriter, which is passenger liability on the buses. When that comes back in writing I will bring you a corrected pack and a firm number. If it does not come back, I will tell you that, and we will go to Bryte re-rated as a school instead. Either way I am not asking you to sign anything on the strength of a limit I am not sure will answer."
