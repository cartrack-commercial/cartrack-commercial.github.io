Second time this insurer has printed a limit that does not fit the vehicle

Play 03

On all 25 King Price vehicle items: "Passenger liability: Private and LDV only R3,000,000" and the same on unauthorised passengers. The fleet is 21 Toyota Quantum Sesfikile 15 and 16 seaters.

You now have documentary proof, which you did not have before The current Old Mutual schedule classifies these vehicles as "MIDI-BUS (seats incl driver not exceeding)" and rates them for Sasria as "Category M3, Minibus, 16 passengers including driver". Class of use Commercial.

And it already gives, inside the premium, on every bus: passenger liability R2,500,000, unauthorised passenger liability R2,500,000, contingent liability R2,500,000.

So King Price is not failing to add cover. It is taking away cover the school already has, and printing a bigger number where the working one used to be. That is the sentence to use.
The precedent, for you not for them Wes-Kaap Busdiens. King Price via VAPS quoted a scheduled bus operator and printed passenger liability on an e-hailing RTU form that would not have answered for commuter, scholar and charter work. We caught it per item on page 10, not off the summary. Same insurer, same shape, different wording. Keep the precedent internal. Put it to King Price as a question about this schedule.
Send exactly this "Please confirm in writing that passenger liability and unauthorised passenger liability under COM58558 respond in respect of Toyota Quantum Sesfikile 15 and 16 seater minibuses used for scholar transport, or reissue the schedule with the 'Private and LDV only' restriction removed and the limit stated per vehicle. For reference, the insured's current schedule classifies these vehicles as MIDI-BUS and rates them Sasria Category M3 Minibus, and a competing quote on the identical fleet offers R2,500,000 passenger liability at a nil excess."

Verbal confirmation is not enough. The restriction is printed 25 times. Only a reissued schedule or an underwriter's letter undoes it.
And the reason you still want King Price The current policy prints "Schools extension: No" and "Cancellation of special event extension: No". So King Price's teacher liability, pupil liability including pupil-to-pupil, R1,000,000 PI covering wrongful expulsion of a scholar, post-traumatic counselling and event cancellation are genuinely new cover, not a match. Its R25,000,000 limit matches the current R5m broadform plus R20m umbrella. Match on limit, gain on scope, for R3,739 a month less than today. That is the deal, once the buses are fixed.

They are in breach of a tracking warranty today

Play 04

This is the strongest telematics lead in the book and it is not a new-insurer condition. It is on the policy they already hold.

Every bus item on the CURRENT schedule
Gearlock: No · Immobiliser: Factory Fitted · Tracking 1: Early Warning System Required · Drivecam: No · Data Dot: No · Alarm system: None
and under Warranties and Endorsements, on every item: "Vehicle Recovery System (the Device) Warranty and Memorandum".
