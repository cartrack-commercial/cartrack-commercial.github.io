The Naked Truth

DO NOT HAND
TO THE CLIENT

Six things that can turn this meeting. Number one is why the Santam quote is not yet final; number four is why the ONE quote is not either.

1
Santam's public liability excludes work away.
Page 71 memoranda. A guarding company has no liability at 107 Trezona Avenue; it has it at the mine and at every client site. As quoted, the section is worth nothing to them.
DOSend it back today: work away in, R5m and R10m priced. Present the corrected quote, not this one.
2
The saving is R2,180, not R2,634.
Santam quoted accidental damage at R150,000 (current R360,000, two sites) and the Samsung S24 at R0 (current R21,000). Restoring both costs about R450/mo. Half of what is left is the add-ons.
SAY"As quoted it is R2,634. Once I put back two things they left out it is about R2,180. I would rather you hear that from me."
3
One Ranger is not comprehensive on the Santam quote.
MOT0003, JN04CFGP, ABSA-financed: cover type "Accidental Damage / Fire and Theft". Every other item is COMP. Nobody noticed because the premium looks normal.
DOCorrect it in the same e-mail as the liability. Do not let it be found by the client.
4
ONE's quote is cheapest by R5,700 and internally inconsistent.
Third-party limit printed on 2 of 27 items, passenger liability on 1 (a Vitz, R1m), unauthorised passenger on 8, contingent on 19, "No" on the rest; night-time and single-vehicle loadings on a patrol fleet; every car rated Sasria M2. It was built fast. Alpina (R5,995/mo) will still counter.
DOSend ONE a one-page correction list and get a reissue. Do not present a schedule that reads differently on every page.
5
The claims history is a condition, and there is a known one.
Santam: "subject to confirmation of claims history." Bryte MOT0012 records a cash settlement on 3 Oct 2025 with proof of repair still outstanding. Undisclosed, that voids the new policy.
DOGet the 3-year claims record from Alpina in writing and send it to Santam before you quote a final number.
6
Nobody has ever quoted the guards themselves.
No group personal accident, no fidelity, no employers' liability on any of the three documents. A guard assaulted on duty, or a guard who steals from the client, is the everyday claim in this industry, and the client's own contracts usually demand both.
SAY"None of the three insurers covers your guards or a theft by one of them. I will price both. That is not part of today's decision; it should be part of next month's."

The objections you'll hit, and the words that answer them
"Two and a half thousand a month isn't worth the admin of moving."
Agreed, and I am not asking you to move for it. I am asking you to move for R2.5 million of passenger cover on every vehicle, an excess of R4,000 instead of R10,000, and a wrongful arrest cover the other new quote dropped. The saving pays for the trackers.
"ONE is R5,000 cheaper than Santam. Why aren't you just recommending ONE?"
Because as printed it insures your guards in the bakkies on one vehicle out of 27, and it adds 5% to the excess for anything that happens between 11pm and 4am, which is your working day. I have asked ONE to fix both. If the reissue comes back with passenger liability on every vehicle and the night loading gone, ONE is the recommendation. If not, Santam has it inside the premium today.
"Hollard is cheaper than Santam."
By R254 a month, and here is what that buys you: only the drivers named on the schedule are covered, third-party drops from R2.5 million to R1 million, no passenger cover, no wrongful arrest, and no theft cover on 24 of your vehicles until every tracker certificate is on file. For a security company with rotating drivers it does not work. Also, their premiums are your Bryte premiums copied to the cent.
"My broker says I've got R5 million third-party."
Your schedule says R5 million on every vehicle. Page 35 of the same document says R2.5 million for any event and R1 million if there is a fire. I have asked Bryte which one applies, and I have asked Santam to give you the R5 million in writing so you actually have what you think you have.
"What about the R1 million public liability, is that enough?"
No, and it is R1 million on all three. The difference is that Santam's, as quoted, excludes work at your clients' premises, and I have already sent it back. I will bring you R5 million and R10 million priced with work away included. That is the number that protects a guarding contract.
"Why do you need my claims history if Santam has quoted already?"
Because the quote says "subject to confirmation of claims history", and your current schedule records a cash settlement on the Ranger KV57DMGP last October. If Santam finds it instead of being told, the policy is voidable. Ten minutes with Alpina and it is done.
