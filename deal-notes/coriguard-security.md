## 🚨 THE FINDINGS (the reason to move is cover, not price)

### 1. Public liability is R1,000,000 on all three, and Santam's excludes work away

A guarding company's liability arises **at the client's premises**, never at 107 Trezona Avenue.

| | Bryte (current) | Santam | Hollard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Limit | R1,000,000 claims-made | R1,000,000 claims-made | R1,000,000 claims-made |
| Retroactive date | 20 Oct 2023 | **20 Oct 2023 retained** ✓ | **not stated → resets to inception** |
| Work away | silent | ⛔ memoranda: **"Public Liability EXCLUDES WORKAWAY"** | silent |
| Legal defence costs | R50,000 (R30) | R50,000 (R5) ✓ | ⛔ **No** |
| Wrongful arrest and defamation | R50,000 (R30) | R50,000 (R5) ✓ | ⛔ **No** |
| Premium | R160.00 | R80.00 | R83.33 |

- **Santam contradicts itself on the same page:** the memoranda says work away is excluded, yet
  the excess table prices *"Work away from the insured premises: 10% of claim, min R1,000 (max
  R25,000)"*. Get work away written INTO the limit before anything is presented. Same shape as
  Lomaen Medical and Vuyos Funerals.
- **Wrongful arrest is core cover for a security company** (guards detain people). Hollard drops
  it and legal defence, and prints no retroactive date, which wipes the 20 Oct 2023 tail.
- **R1,000,000 is thin for guards on a mine site.** Ask Santam to price R5,000,000 and
  R10,000,000 with work away included. That is the lift that makes the pack worth presenting.

### 2. Nobody insures the guards in the vehicles today, Santam does

Bryte: **"Additional Passenger Liability: No"** on all 25 items, and the armoured FJ Cruiser used
for mine patrols carries an explicit note: *"Passenger Liability is excluded."* These vehicles move
supervisors and guards to and from posts every shift. **Santam gives passenger liability R2,500,000
AND unauthorised passenger liability R2,500,000 on every one of the 25 vehicles, inside the
premium.** Hollard offers neither. This is the decisive motor point and the gain to lead with.

### 4. Third-party liability on the motor section: Hollard is R1,000,000

- Bryte's item pages print **Sub-Section B R5,000,000** on every vehicle, but the motor section
  wording (p35) caps it: *fire/explosion R1,000,000; any other event and the aggregate
  R2,500,000.* **The schedule and the wording disagree**; ask Bryte which applies.
- Santam **R2,500,000** on every item (matches Bryte's wording). Ask for R5,000,000.
- Hollard **R1,000,000**. A fifth of what the client thinks they have.

### 5. Hollard is benched, on five counts

1. **Nominated drivers only:** *"Only drivers listed on the schedule will be covered by this
   insurance."* A security company with rotating supervisors and patrol drivers cannot run on a
   named-driver policy.
2. **Third-party R1,000,000, no passenger liability at all.**
3. **No theft or hijack cover until a tracker is fitted on every vehicle over R100,000** (24 of
   25; only the 2002 Colt at R69,500 is under). *"Tracking Certificate Received: No"* on all 25.
4. **"Collared Burner" loss-ratio endorsement**: target claims ratio 60%, additional premium at
   70%. The price is not the price.
5. **It is not a re-rating.** Hollard's item premiums equal Bryte's item totals **to the cent on
   15 of the 22 items matched** (Polo R1,357.52, FJ R3,543.68, Ranger R2,400.43, BMW R5,818.21 and
   so on); the other seven differ by exactly the credit shortfall premium Hollard dropped. The R90
   per vehicle "Vehicle Control Management" sits inside. Also: every second-hand vehicle needs an
   inspection certificate within 48 hours, the combined liability has no retro date and no wrongful
   arrest, and the office cover is Bryte's list restructured (BAR R101,999 + solar section R59,807
   = Bryte's R161,806 exactly).
