## 3. THE DECISIVE COVER: e-hailing, fare-paying passengers, rental

### 3a. AC&E: does it respond to an accident on an Uber/Bolt trip?

**A full-text search of all 32 pages found NO mention of e-hailing, Uber, Bolt, fare, fare-paying, reward, or hire-and-reward use.** What the quote does say:

- **Business description (ACE p1):** *"Rent-to-own and also short term and long term vehicle rentals"*. E-hailing is not disclosed.
- **Class of use, both blocks (ACE p7, p11):** *"Private & Business"*. In SA motor underwriting this class does not include carrying fare-paying passengers for reward; e-hailing and taxi use are normally a separate, declared class.
- **Vehicle definition (ACE p13, Motor General Condition 6a):** *"Private type motor car (including station wagons, minibuses (excluding taxis) motorised caravans and the like ...) designed to seat 10 persons or less"*. Taxis are carved out of the definition the whole fleet is rated under.
- **AC&E Assist exclusions (ACE p29, item 3):** *"Motorcycles, taxicabs and limousines"* excluded from the LDV/HCV roadside programme.
- **Duty of disclosure (ACE p3, Annexure A, clause 2 and 2.1):** the company *"may void the whole or any part of this policy ... as from inception ... or rejection of a claim"* for *"Misrepresentation of any material and personal details that could affect the risk."* Use for e-hailing is material.
- **Passenger Liability R2,500,000 (ACE p7, p11; p15 "Passenger Liability: R2 500 000.00 (No cover for HCV or Motorcycles)")** is a generic motor extension. **Nothing says it covers fare-paying passengers.** Unauthorised passenger R2.5m, TP R2.5m, contingent R2.5m, parking facilities R2.5m, fire/explosion R1m (ACE p7, p15).
- The AC&E Commercial policy wording itself is NOT in the 32 pages (Annexure A says it "must be read in conjunction with the applicable wording", ACE p3). Any hire-or-reward exclusion would sit there.

**Conclusion: as quoted, AC&E has been told this is a rental fleet used privately and for business. An accident during a paid Uber/Bolt trip is at real risk of repudiation (use outside the declared class plus non-disclosure), and the R2.5m passenger liability cannot be assumed to respond to a fare-paying passenger.** Same shape as the Wes-Kaap King Price and Gateway King Price passenger-liability gates. **Gate: AC&E must confirm IN WRITING that the 128 vehicles are covered while used for e-hailing (Uber/Bolt) by non-employee drivers, with passenger liability responding to fare-paying passengers, and re-rate if needed. Without that the price is not comparable.**

### 3b. AC&E: theft by the renter (decisive for a rental / rent-to-own book)

- **Absconding Clause (ACE p15), quoted in full:** *"No theft cover for vehicles when: - Hired through false pretences, - Conversion occurs (person who originally obtained possession of property lawfully later deals with it as if it were their own, in a manner inconsistent with the owner's rights), - Hiring done fraudulently, - Failure to return leased vehicle occurs, - Absconding with the vehicle occurs"*.
- **"Theft is subject to Forcible & Violent Entry into/Exist: Yes, As per policy wording"** (ACE p7, p11). A renter has the keys; there is no forcible entry.
- **Vehicle Protection Guarantee (ACE p12, condition 3):** where an anti-theft device is required or declared, *"it is the responsibility of the insured to prove that the security system as indicated or required was fitted and in working order"*, failing which the claim can be turned down; a satellite tracker must have a self-activating mechanism and a valid recovery agreement.
- So **AC&E pays nothing when a driver/renter disappears with a car**, which is the characteristic loss of a rent-to-own book. Recovery depends entirely on the tracker.

### 3c. RTU: what the current policy says

- **Built for e-hailing:** product name "E-Hailing" (p1). Every item records **"Unexpired Uber/Taxify Vehicle Inspection Certificate: Yes"** (136 of 136) and **"Operators vehicle is registered with: More than one operator"** (136 of 136). Additional-excess table names *"Accident in which no fare paying passengers are on board"* (p107), later removed (p108). PrDP condition on 81 items speaks of *"transport fare-paying passengers"* (e.g. p3). Taxi Violence exclusion (p3 and each endorsed item).
- **Passenger liability: NO LIMIT IS PRINTED ANYWHERE in 114 pages.** The only reference is a jurisdiction clause: *"There is NO COVER in respect of judgments delivered or obtained in the first instance ... otherwise than by a court of competent jurisdiction within the Republic of South Africa"* (p3 and 80 other item pages). **Third-party limit: also not printed.** "Selected Policy Level Benefits" is empty (p107). **Get the RTU wording or a written confirmation of the passenger and TP limits before claiming any gain or loss against AC&E's R2.5m.**
- 🚨 **RTU also has a rental problem.** Item endorsement *"Theft/Hijack Condition and Taxi Rental / Hiring"* (p3, on 81 items): *"There will be no Theft or Hijacking cover under this policy if the insured vehicle is voluntarily handed over to the alleged thief or hijacker without threat of violence. There is also no cover under this policy if the insured vehicle is rented or hired out to a Third Party and is no longer under the control of the insured or a driver employed by the insured."*
  - Every item lists the owner as **not** the regular driver, and the principal driver's occupation as **"Self Employed"** (136 of 136). On a rent-to-own model the driver is a renter, not an employee, so this clause can bite on **all** cover, not just theft.
  - **Section "DESCRIPTION OF USE" (p107):** *"Use for social, domestic and pleasure purposes and use for the business or occupation of the Insured, excluding hiring-out, racing or other contests ..."*. **Hiring-out is excluded on the current policy too.**
  - **"Rent To Own Special Endorsement: Rent to own vehicles policy conditions are not applicable to this vehicle"** (item 3, p4) and *"... not applicable to this cover"* (policy level, p108). So whatever RTU's rent-to-own conditions are, they are switched OFF on this policy.
  - The item endorsement block prints on items 3 to 460 (81 items, p3 to p80); **items 462 to 536 (55 items, p81 to p106) print no item endorsements at all.** Confirm they still apply.
- **Driver / operator conditions on RTU:**

  - Overloading and roadworthiness; drunk driving rejection (p3).

  - Tracking warranty (p107): see section 5.
  - **"Own Damage Cover is excluded until the inspection form has been received"** (p107).

### 3d. Bottom line on the decisive cover

**Neither document cleanly insures the business as it actually runs.** RTU is at least an e-hailing product (Uber inspection certificates, operator registration, fare-paying wording) but excludes hiring-out and switches off rent-to-own conditions; AC&E is a private-and-business commercial motor quote for a "rental" company with no e-hailing disclosure at all, an explicit absconding/failure-to-return exclusion, and a "taxis excluded" vehicle definition. **AC&E is not a like-for-like replacement until e-hailing use is disclosed and accepted in writing.**
