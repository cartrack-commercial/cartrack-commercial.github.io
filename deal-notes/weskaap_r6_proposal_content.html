How ONE prices the fleet, and what that means for you ONE charges one fleet premium, R390,000 a year, rather than 51 separate rates. It asks for a final fleet list with values before the policy starts, will review Sasria on that list, caps any single vehicle at R650,000 unless declared (only the Iveco is, at R1,280,643), excludes hired-in vehicles, and does not cover a newly bought vehicle automatically. Every addition must be declared the day it arrives. Two of your MAN 65-seaters have no registration numbers on the Santam schedule; ONE will need them.
Trackers, or no theft cover on ten vehicles ONE requires a monitored early-warning tracking and recovery unit on every vehicle worth R500,000 or more, and on any Hilux, Fortuner or Quantum regardless of value, failing which theft is not covered. On your fleet that is the Hilux, Fortuner, Quantum, both Scanias, both MAN 65-seaters, the Irizar, the Iveco and the Prado: ten units. Neither schedule shows a device fitted today. Cartrack units on those ten meet the condition and give you live location on the buses that carry your passengers.
