## 🚨 THE FINDING: King Price restricts passenger liability to "Private and LDV only"

Gateway runs **21 Toyota Quantum Sesfikile 15 and 16 seaters**. That is the business: moving
special-needs children to and from school every day.

On all 25 King Price vehicle items the schedule reads, verbatim:

> **Passenger liability: Private and LDV only** R3,000,000
> **Unauthorised passengers liability: Private and LDV only** R3,000,000

A Quantum Sesfikile is a minibus. It is neither a private car nor an LDV. On a plain reading the
R3,000,000 printed against every bus **will not respond**, and the printed limit makes it look as
though it will.

**This is the same shape as Wes-Kaap Busdiens**, where King Price via VAPS printed a passenger
liability limit on an e-hailing RTU form that would not have answered for a scheduled bus operator.
Second time on the same insurer, same trap. **Get it confirmed or corrected in writing before any
figure reaches the school.**

## Recommendation

**King Price, conditional on one thing: passenger liability extended in writing to the minibuses.**

It is R1,542 a month more than Bryte and it is the only quote that understands what it is insuring:
R25,000,000 of liability with teacher and pupil cover built in, on a school for special-needs
children. That is the cover that answers when a child is hurt at school.

But as written, the passenger liability that answers when a child is hurt **on the bus** is
restricted to a vehicle class Gateway does not operate. Both cannot be left unresolved.

**Order of play:**

1. **To King Price:** confirm in writing that passenger and unauthorised passenger liability respond
   for Toyota Quantum Sesfikile 15 and 16 seaters used for scholar transport, or reissue with the
   restriction removed. Quote the Wes-Kaap precedent internally, not to them.
2. **To King Price:** price employers liability, glass and fidelity, all three currently excluded.
3. **To Bryte:** re-rate as a school. "Industry: Unspecified" is why the schools extensions are
   missing and why legal defence sits at R50,000.
4. **Bench Auto & General.** Dearest, and the smallest policy of the three.
5. **Get the Alpha `ALPHACOM204975` schedule** so we can say whether any of this is an improvement
   on what they have, rather than only how the three compare with each other.

Do not present a price until item 1 comes back. If King Price fixes the passenger liability it is a
clear recommendation. If it refuses, the decision becomes Bryte re-rated as a school, and that is a
different conversation with the client.
