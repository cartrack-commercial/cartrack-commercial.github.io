## 🚨 THE FINDING: ONE is charging fire on the buses twice

Santam's fire section (p7) lists four items under column 5: diesel in a bulk tank R150,000; office
contents R59,400; plant, machinery and stock R659,340; and **"Sasria cover for the busses, depot
risk only" R7,923,960 at R0.00**. That last line exists only so bus Sasria could be charged under
the fire section; it carries no fire premium and the buses are insured under motor. Subtotal
**R8,792,700**.

ONE's fire section (p9) insures **"Miscellaneous R8,942,700"** = that R8,792,700 subtotal plus the
R150,000 diesel again, at **R1,341.41/mo**, plus **P&M R659,340 at R98.90** (already inside the
subtotal), plus a separate **office contents section R59,400** (also inside it). Sasria F2 on the
R8.94m: **R350.82**. So ONE prices R7,923,960 of buses as a building, and prices the diesel,
the P&M and the office contents twice.

Real property at 8 Uitspan Street = diesel R150,000 + P&M and stock R659,340 + office contents
R59,400 = **R868,740**. Corrected ONE fire ≈ R121 + Sasria ≈ R32; remove ≈ **R1,664/mo**. That is
where "fire corrected" above comes from. **Get the ONE commercial quote reissued** with fire at
R809,340 (diesel + P&M) and office contents at R59,400.

## ⛔ The gate has not moved: is the R5m passenger liability FARE-PAYING?

The fleet quote says "Passenger Liability R5 000 000" and "Unauthorised Passenger Liability
R5 000 000" and nothing else. Five rounds of this file turned on one question, whether the
passenger cover responds for a scheduled commuter, WCED scholar and charter operator. ONE
Underwriting Managers' own July quote (TOURS/1267000) needed a **dedicated fare-paying passenger
liability section** added in round 2 before it matched Santam. This HCV quote has no such
section. **Written confirmation, on the schedule, that the R5,000,000 covers fare-paying
passengers on all 30 buses and the minibus, with the seat basis stated, before this goes to the
client as final.**
