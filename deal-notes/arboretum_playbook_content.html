Play 02 · why Hollard is benched

The cheapest quote has excluded the school

Play 02

Hollard is R577 a month cheaper than ONE. Here is what that R577 buys, and it is the clearest bench decision in the book this year.

01
Endorsement LIA_011, the "Schools exception"
No indemnity for any claim arising out of child molestation or sexual abuse of any kind, wrongful or excessive discipline of any kind, or bullying or harassment of any kind. Those three plus pupil-to-pupil are close to the entire liability exposure of a primary school.
"I am not going to quote you a price on a policy that excludes bullying at a primary school."
02
Pupil-to-pupil liability excluded
Stated in the section memo, separately from LIA_011. Child injures child is the single most likely liability claim a primary school ever makes.
"Your current policy includes pupil liability. This one names it as excluded."
03
R1,000,000 limit, claims-made, retro = inception
Against R1m plus a R20m top-up today. And a retroactive date of 01 Oct 2026 means every year the school has already operated becomes uninsured for liability on the day it moves.
"Everything that happened before we sign stops being covered. That is the deal."
04
The umbrella probably cannot attach
Hollard quotes Umbrella Liability R17,500,000. Its own Schedule of Liability Limits requires an underlying limit of R2,500,000. The underlying Combined Liability quoted is R1,000,000. Same defect we found on the Western quote for Powerflow.
Put it to ASI Blue in writing. Do not assume it is a typo in our favour.
05
No legal defence, no wrongful arrest, no products
Additional Statutory Legal Defence Costs: No. Additional Wrongful Arrest and Defamation: No. Products and Defective Workmanship: No. Santam carries R500,000 and R250,000.
"A school gets accused before it gets sued. Defence costs are the cover you use first."
06
Employers liability cut to R1,000,000
Santam gives R5,000,000 on a claims-made basis with a retroactive date of 1 April 2008. Eighteen years of tail, gone, and the limit cut by 80 percent.
This one matters for the staff, not the pupils. Say it that way.
Two Hollard PDFs, same quote number The 10:28 issue was superseded at 11:07 with "corrected Combined Liability to the correct R1m limit". The premium did not change when the limit did. Work off the 11:07 file only, and ask ASI Blue what R23,516.03 actually buys if the limit moved without the price moving.

Play 05 · battlecard

In the room

Play 05
"Hollard is cheaper. Why are you not showing me that one?"
"I am showing it to you. I am also showing you that it excludes bullying, excessive discipline, sexual abuse and pupil-to-pupil claims at a primary school, and that its liability limit is one million against twenty. It is five hundred and seventy seven rand a month cheaper. I would not put a school on it, and I would rather tell you that than let you find out at a claim."
"What are we actually saving?"
"I cannot tell you yet, and I am not going to guess. Your schedule came to us with the premiums removed. What I can tell you is exactly what each of these three covers against what you have, and on the two lines that decide a school, buses and liability, only one of them holds your position. If you give me the current premium I will have the saving to you in a day."
"Our broker has been with us for years."
"That is worth something and I am not asking you to throw it away. What I would ask is why nobody has ever put fidelity cover in front of you. You collect fees, you run a tuck shop, you hold fundraisers, and there is no fidelity section on your policy or on any of these three quotes. That is not a Marsh failing so much as a gap the whole market has left sitting."
"Bryte is a big name. Why is it bottom of your list?"
"Because it is five and a half thousand a month more than ONE, and it has passenger liability switched off on all eleven items, including both Quantums and the Iveco. It also has your fourteen seater recorded as a ten seater and a trailer recorded as a 2007 minibus. It is not a bad insurer. It is a quote that has not been built carefully."
Close like this "My recommendation is ONE, at twenty four thousand and ninety three a month, and I am not asking you to sign anything today. I have gone back to them with five items, and the most important is that your pre-primary and FTRRI are named on the policy alongside the primary school. When that comes back in writing I will bring you a corrected pack. If it does not come back, I will tell you that too."
