Play 01
Read before you speak to the client

Win on the findings, not the price

We have no number to beat yet: the premium summary and commission disclosure on the copy are wiped mid-figure, and FSP (Meldon) has not quoted. Don't quote a saving. Lead with two things the current broker should have fixed and didn't.

1The R100,000 excess. Old Mutual's survey requirements at 13/15 and 16 Rising Sun St are outstanding, so every damage claim there carries 10% min R100,000 instead of R25,000. Ask: "Weet julle daarvan?" Most clients don't.
2No business interruption. R93.7m of plant and stock at one address. A fire stops the business, and nothing pays salaries, rent or lost profit. Ask for turnover and gross profit; that is the BI quote.
3No machinery breakdown, no power surge. Presses break down internally far more often than they burn. Surge is switched off, and grid failure is excluded across the policy.
4Get the real premium. Debit order amount, or the original PDF from Old Mutual. Without it we can't show a saving.
5Then the market. Like for like plus BI, MB and surge as options. Keep the PL retro date of 1 July 2003.
The question that sells the file
"As 16 Rising Sun Street afbrand, hoe lank tot julle weer druk?"
Whatever they answer, the current policy pays for the presses and stock and nothing for those months.
Who you are up against The broker of record is CDK Events Solutions (Pty) Ltd, agency 8669368. The name reads like an events business, so check its FSP status before the meeting. Don't attack them; let the outstanding survey and the missing BI make the point.

When the real premium comes backWhat it tells you
Under R50,000
Cheaply placed; the market will struggle to beat it on price. Sell the cover gaps and the survey, and quote BI and MB as additions.
R55,000 to R70,000
As projected. A like-for-like re-market can compete; the survey clearance is the lever on the fire rate.
Over R75,000
Fire is likely loaded for the open RRs or the claims record. Strongest case to move once the survey is cleared; ask for the claims history.
Why fire swings the range Fire is about half the premium and the rate depends on the survey. With the RR items open, Old Mutual may be rating it at the top of the range; once they are cleared, a new insurer may rate it lower. Clearing the survey is worth money in the quote, not just in the excess.
