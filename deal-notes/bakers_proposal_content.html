Before any of that is read as a price

Both your motor policies say they do not cover vehicles carrying hazardous materials

Both OUTsurance schedules record your business as "Service & Repair, Transport, Goods" and both print this under Fleet special conditions:

From your own schedules
"OUTsurance does not provide cover in the following instances:
  * for code 4 (demolished) vehicles
  * when the vehicle transports money
  * when the vehicle transports hazardous materials
  * when the vehicle transports people …"

The goods in transit submission prepared for you on 14 September describes what you carry as:

⚠ From the goods in transit submission
"General Goods, Diesel, Petrol, Illuminated Paraffin, Jet Fuel, FMCG, Scrap Metal and Scrap Batteries."
Diesel, petrol, paraffin and jet fuel are hazardous materials. On the face of these documents, the vehicles carrying them sit outside the motor policies that are supposed to cover them.
And the contradiction is in the insurer's own words, twice The same schedules sell you environmental impairment liability of R5,000,000 per vehicle on 52 of your trucks. The conditions attaching to that cover read:
"owners, drivers and operators must be familiar with legislation and other requirements relating to the transport of dangerous substances and goods … a professional driving permit for vehicles transporting dangerous goods will also be required."
One page excludes hazardous materials. Another prices dangerous goods liability and tells you which permits your drivers must hold. Both are on the same document.
How ONE treats the same load ONE's goods in transit quotation names your cargo as diesel, petrol, paraffin and jet fuel and accepts it in writing, on condition that it moves under SANS and road traffic rules, in licensed vehicles, with experienced drivers. Its motor quotation prints no hazardous materials exclusion, but records "goods carried: TBA". If ONE is ever taken forward, the fuel must be declared on the motor proposal before anything is signed.
What we have asked for A written confirmation from OUTsurance, reflected on both schedules, that either the hazardous materials exclusion does not apply to your fleet, or that it does.

We are not asking you to change anything on the strength of this. We are asking you not to decide anything until it is answered. 215 vehicles and a projected bonus of R562,632 turn on the answer, and so does the size of the spill cover you need to buy.
