Diesel, petrol, paraffin and jet fuel are hazardous materials. On the face of these two documents, the vehicles carrying them sit outside the motor policy that is supposed to cover them.

What we have asked for A written confirmation from OUTsurance, reflected on the schedule, that either the hazardous materials exclusion does not apply to your fleet, or that it does.

We are not asking you to change anything on the strength of this. We are asking you not to decide anything until it is answered, because if the exclusion applies as written, the question of which quotation is cheaper does not arise.

The cover that decides a tanker fleet

Spill clean-up, and why we cannot recommend the motor move yet

On a general freight fleet the decisive cover is the third-party limit. On a tanker fleet it is what pays to clean up a spill, because that is the loss that runs into millions before anyone has counted the vehicle.

Two problems with the quoted version 1. The limit is one fifth. R1,000,000 against R5,000,000, for a clean-up on a national road.

2. It sits only on the truck section. Alpha's liability tables for trailers over 3,500kg, for light commercials and for private vehicles each carry third-party liability of R5,000,000 and no clean-up line at all. On a tanker fleet the product is in the trailer, not the horse. A spill from a tanker trailer may fall outside an extension written only against the truck.
One thing to know about the cover you have The OUTsurance environmental liability is written on a claims-made basis with a retroactive date of 30 June 2026. It reaches back about six weeks. Anything that happened before that date is not covered for pollution on either document. That is worth knowing whichever way you go.
The product that actually answers, now quoted Envirosure Spill Protect Plan, Transport, underwritten by Centriq, at R15,890.00 a month. Cover for the exact goods you carry, diesel, petrol, illuminated paraffin, jet fuel, general goods, scrap metal and scrap batteries:

Where this leaves Thursday

What we recommend, and what we are not recommending yet

Something worth knowing before any motor decision Your OUTsurance schedule projects a Business OUTbonus of R307,010.54, payable to you on 7 August 2028, based on your current claims ratio. Moving the motor account forfeits it. We are telling you that even though it argues against a change, because you should hear it from us rather than find it later. It is a claims-ratio benefit, so it is not guaranteed, but it is real money and it belongs in the decision.
Our recommendation
  1. 1Hold the motor account where it is for now. Not because the quote is poor, it is now about 30% cheaper per truck, but because its spill clean-up cover is one fifth of the real product and sits on the wrong section.
  2. 2Get the hazardous materials answer in writing. This is the one that could change everything.
  3. 3Tell us what insures the other 184 vehicles. Alpha's fleet count has grown from 183 to 214 in the last few days, and OUTsurance still covers 30.
  4. 4Reconfirm the spill cover quote, which has lapsed, so you can see a firm limit and a nil excess option.
  5. 5Move the goods in transit once all three entities are named and the high-risk-cargo excess is confirmed. That saving is real and it is about R29,454 a year.
One operational item that is worth money either way The new goods in transit terms make theft cover on any load over R500,000 conditional on live satellite tracking, operational at the time of the claim, or a convoy of at least three vehicles. A full fuel load is comfortably over R500,000, so in practice that is every trip, and it applies to the trailer as much as the truck, because the cargo travels with the trailer. That is 29 trucks and 119 trailers. We can quote the units alongside whichever policies you end up with, and your ten-year claims record would also support driver scoring.
Smaller things we will clean up Your registered address appears as 15 Nereeniging Road, Alberton 1449 on one policy and 15 Vereeniging Road, Alberton 1451 on the others. The OUTsurance depot record allows a maximum of 27 vehicles against 30 scheduled. The new goods in transit quote carries no inception date and describes your loss history as clean for three years, which does not match the motor record. None of these change the advice, all of them should be right before anything is signed.
