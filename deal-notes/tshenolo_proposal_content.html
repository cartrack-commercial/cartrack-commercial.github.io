The finding that comes before any decision about price

Most of the fleet has no theft cover today

Your current policy makes theft and hijack cover conditional. This is not a recommendation or a suggestion in the wording. It is a condition precedent to liability, which means the insurer does not have to pay if it is not met.

Tracking Devices Warranty, value over R500,000 "In the event of loss or damage to the vehicle as a result of theft and/or hijacking it is precedent to the liability of the Company, that the insured vehicle is fitted with an early warning tracking and recovery system", linked at all times, operational and armed, with the subscription kept up to date. Two further clauses on the same schedule require one device and two devices on specified vehicles.

Why a shortfall matters more than it looks If a vehicle is insured for less than it is worth, the insurer applies average and pays the same proportion of the loss. On Alpha's heavy vehicle values a total loss would settle at roughly 92.7 cents in the rand. Natsure has copied your register to the rand on four of the five classes and is short only on the private cars. Both must be corrected to R79,394,630 before anything is signed, and we have asked for that in writing.
One more thing to fix on your own schedule The index page of your current policy prints the motor sum insured as R61,354,333, while the asset register four pages later totals R79,394,630. The register is the figure the premium is calculated on. We have asked your current broker to correct the index, because a schedule that misstates its own largest sum insured is exactly what gets argued at claim stage.
Two vehicles, two different years

A BMW X5 is quoted as a 2026 by one insurer and a 2023 by the other; a BMW M135i as a 2025 and a 2024. Your schedule settles both. We have asked for the corrections rather than picking the higher figure, because an inflated year inflates the premium as surely as a low one under-insures the car.

Sasria is declared on the wrong basis

Your fifteen private cars are declared to Sasria as Goods Vehicles, and the fleet declaration of R59,436,465 is short of the heavy fleet by R1,738,186, which is exactly the two buses. Sasria is a statutory rate on declared values, so this is worth correcting on whichever policy runs.

The fleet itself is young, and that helps you Every vehicle on the register is 2019 or newer: two from 2019, nine from 2020, one from 2021, seven from 2022, six from 2023, twelve from 2024 and eight from 2025, plus a 2025 Volvo FH 520 truck-tractor at R2,617,300. That matters because Natsure requires an inspection certificate for every vehicle ten years and older, and on your fleet that condition affects no vehicle at all. It is one of the few conditions on either quotation that you have already satisfied.

There is a real saving here on either reading, and it is large. We are not recommending that you bind either quotation today, for three reasons that are all fixable within a fortnight.

  • 1
    Tracking, before anything else
    Fit and activate approved tracking across the fleet and get the insurer to confirm the warranty is met in writing. Until that is done your theft and hijack cover is conditional on something your own schedule says is missing, on R58,257,591 of vehicles.
  • 2
    Get liability priced
    Public, products and employers' liability from both markets. A waste business working inside hospitals should not be carrying only a motor third party limit.
  • 3
    Correct the sums insured
    Both quotations re-rated to your register total of R79,394,630, and the two disputed vehicle model years resolved against your schedule.
  • 4
    Decide on the extras deliberately
    Car hire, roadside assistance and credit shortfall, worth R46,307 a month between them. Keep them, priced by the new insurer, or drop them knowingly.
  • 5
    Release the claims record
    Both quotations are conditional on three years' claims experience confirmed in writing. Until that is provided neither price is firm, and we will not present a number to you as final that the insurer can still move.
Bottom line On the figures in front of us Alpha is the better price and Natsure is the better policy. Alpha is R38,547 a month cheaper than Natsure, but Natsure carries double the third party limit, adds clean-up cover on the trucks and has quoted your fleet at the right values. Once liability is priced by both and the sums insured are corrected, we expect the gap to narrow and we will come back to you with a final recommendation. The tracking point should not wait for any of that.
