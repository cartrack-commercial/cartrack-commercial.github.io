The cover that decides this renewal

What answers when your truck hurts someone

Third party liability is what pays when one of your vehicles injures a person or damages someone else's property. On a loaded fuel tanker it is the most important number in the policy. Santam sets it by vehicle class. Both markets cap at R5,000,000 — Petrosure have confirmed that is their ceiling and cannot be exceeded, and MiWay quotes R5,000,000 on the fleet and only R1,000,000 on your trailers.

Why this is the one we will not let through A truck-tractor pulling a full four-compartment diesel tanker is the largest thing you put on a national road. Today it carries R10 million of third party cover. Petrosure's maximum is R5 million, and that is their ceiling, not their opening position. A single serious accident — several vehicles, injuries, a fire — passes R5 million, and everything above it comes out of the business. We have asked them to at least lift the quotation to their R5 million and price it, so you can see the whole picture.
MiWay hits the same ceiling, and goes further down: its trailers carry only R1,000,000 of third party cover against the R5,000,000 you hold today. Neither market can replace the R10 million on your truck-tractors. That single line is the reason this renewal stays where it is.

Agreed value is confirmed. Petrosure has confirmed in writing that your specialist units — the GRW, Afrit and Trailer & Tanker Technology tankers — can be written on Agreed Value, subject to accepting the values at inception. The four light vehicles stay on retail, exactly as they are at Santam today.
And your liability history is protected Your public liability is written on a claims made basis with a retroactive date of 20 August 2021. Petrosure has confirmed that date will be carried across unchanged, subject to proof of uninterrupted cover. That protects five years of your past work — it was the single biggest technical risk in a move like this, and it is closed.

The gap that exists on both · and matters most

Nothing here pays to clean up a spill

This is not a comparison point. It is true of the policy you have now and of the one you were offered, and it is the single largest uninsured exposure we can see in your business.

What the documents actually say
  • Santam. Your schedule reads "Environmental impairment liability — not included" against every one of the 27 vehicles.
  • Petrosure. No environmental or pollution section anywhere in the quotation. It carries "forecourt negligence" — which is fuel dispensed at a filling station, not fuel on a road.
  • MiWay. "Environmental transport liability — excluded" on the fleet, and the goods section adds that there is no cover for environmental liability unless it is selected on the vehicle section. It is not selected.
What that means If one of your tankers rolls and diesel reaches soil, a storm-water drain or a water course, the clean-up, the rehabilitation, the monitoring and the regulator's costs are yours. Those costs are not proportional to the load — a single incident at the wrong place can run to many times the value of the vehicle.
What we are doing about it
  • We asked Petrosure to quote it. They have confirmed they do not offer environmental impairment or pollution liability — and pointed us to Envirosure, a specialist market.
  • We are asking Santam the same question on your existing policy, so you are not waiting on any re-quote to close a gap you have today.
  • We are taking it to Envirosure as a standalone placement. That is normal for fuel hauliers and it is a small premium against the exposure.
Our view Of everything in this review, this is the item we would fix first — ahead of the premium, ahead of the re-quote. It is the only exposure here capable of ending the business rather than costing it money.
Two smaller ones, while we are here Credit shortfall is on none of the three — Petrosure have confirmed they do not offer it and MiWay excludes it — and Investec Bank and Redtree Capital interests are noted on your fleet. Passenger liability is excluded on MiWay.
Roadside assistance: Petrosure can only give it below 3.5 tons, so your 23 heavy units get none. Santam includes breakdown towing, towing in and outside the RSA and wreckage removal on all of them. MiWay is the strongest here — towing and recovery R100,000, mechanical breakdown tow R75,000 and side tank spill R250,000.
