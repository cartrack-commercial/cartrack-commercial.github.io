The cover that decides this renewal

What answers when your truck hurts someone

Third party liability is what pays when one of your vehicles injures a person or damages someone else's property. On a loaded fuel tanker it is the most important number in the policy. Santam sets it by vehicle class. Petrosure sets one figure for everything.

Why this is the one we will not let through A truck-tractor pulling a full four-compartment diesel tanker is the largest thing you put on a national road. Today it carries R10 million of third party cover. The quotation offers R2.5 million. A single serious accident — several vehicles, injuries, a fire — passes R2.5 million quickly, and everything above it comes out of the business. We have asked Petrosure to re-quote on your existing class structure.
One vehicle is under-insured on the quotation MG45VLGP (2025 Shacman X3000 480) is shown at R1,516,105 on the Petrosure quotation and R1,816,105 on your Santam schedule — R300,000 short. On a write-off you would carry that difference. It is on the list to correct.
Also unconfirmed: how a total loss would be settled Santam insures 23 of your 27 units on Agreed Value, with a 10% variance. The Petrosure quotation does not state a basis at all. Retail book value means very little for a custom-built GRW or Trailer & Tanker Technology tanker — agreed value is what makes those figures real. We have asked for it in writing.

The gap that exists on both · and matters most

Nothing here pays to clean up a spill

This is not a comparison point. It is true of the policy you have now and of the one you were offered, and it is the single largest uninsured exposure we can see in your business.

What the documents actually say
  • Santam. Your schedule reads "Environmental impairment liability — not included" against every one of the 27 vehicles.
  • Petrosure. No environmental or pollution section anywhere in the quotation. It carries "forecourt negligence" — which is fuel dispensed at a filling station, not fuel on a road.
What that means If one of your tankers rolls and diesel reaches soil, a storm-water drain or a water course, the clean-up, the rehabilitation, the monitoring and the regulator's costs are yours. Those costs are not proportional to the load — a single incident at the wrong place can run to many times the value of the vehicle.
What we are doing about it
  • We have asked Petrosure to quote environmental impairment liability, either on the motor section or standalone.
  • We will ask Santam the same question on your existing policy, so you are not waiting on a re-quote to close a gap you have today.
  • Where neither will write it, it is placed as a separate environmental impairment policy. That is normal for fuel hauliers and it is a small premium against the exposure.
Our view Of everything in this review, this is the item we would fix first — ahead of the premium, ahead of the re-quote. It is the only exposure here capable of ending the business rather than costing it money.
Two smaller ones, while we are here Credit shortfall is on neither policy, and Investec Bank and Redtree Capital interests are noted on your fleet. Roadside assistance is on your Santam policy and not on the quotation.
