The most important page in this document

Your liability policy excludes what you do

Your business is described on your own schedule as "import of x-ray machines, marketing, installing and repair of new machines". Every installation and every repair happens at a customer's premises. Your public liability section carries this endorsement:

Work Away Exclusion — endorsement 001, on your current policy "The Company will not indemnify the Insured in respect of liability consequent upon death, injury or damage caused by or through or in connection with work done away from the Insured premises."

In plain terms: if something goes wrong while your team is installing or repairing a machine at a hospital or a practice, your liability policy does not respond. There is also no products liability and no defective workmanship cover on the schedule at all.

And what would be left uninsured R73,266,429 of stock in trade. R75,000,000 of annual carry at R3,000,000 a load. R10,000,000 of liability including the work-away cover discussed on page 02. R1.86m of test equipment and roughly R1.1m of electronics. Insuring eleven vehicles worth R3.89 million and leaving that outside is not a smaller version of the same programme — it is a different one, and it does not cover the way this business actually loses money.
If you want the saving larger
  • Roadside assist is R1,330.00 a month on the Bryte quotation. You currently carry motor assistance on three vehicles at R39.82.
  • Office Buddy is R225.00 a month — a value-added service, not insurance.
  • Strip both and the saving becomes R10,275.53 a month, or R123,306 a year. Bryte's roadside is a commercial-vehicle product and genuinely different — but at R15,960 a year it is your call, not ours.
Two gaps that exist either way
  • No business interruption cover — not on the Bryte quotation and not on your current policy either. With R73.3m of stock, one address and import lead times, we would like to quote it.
  • Stock at your customer's site is covered for a maximum of four weeks after delivery and excludes accidental damage. For an installer of x-ray machines that is exactly the wrong window. We have asked for it extended.
