If you place the vehicles alone

What would be left uninsured

This is not an argument for spending more. It is a list, so that whatever you decide, you decide it knowing what sits outside the policy.

The four that matter most
  • R73,266,429 of stock in trade. Your imported machines, at your premises. This is the single largest figure anywhere in your programme and it is the whole fire section.
  • R75,000,000 of annual carry, R3,000,000 per load. Machines in transit by road, rail and air — including on third-party carriers up to a quarter of the carry.
  • R10,000,000 of liability, including work away, plus R5,000,000 of products and defective workmanship — the cover that answers when an installation you completed causes harm.
  • R1.86m of test equipment and roughly R1.1m of electronics — the Unfors tester, the QA equipment, the phantoms and dose meters, the IBA MAGIMIX, Synapse 3D and about fifty-five laptops.
Things worth fixing either way
  • Stock at your customer's site is covered for a maximum of four weeks after delivery and excludes accidental damage. Installations often run longer. We are asking for the period extended and accidental damage added.
  • There is no business interruption cover on either quotation, because it was not asked for. With R73m of stock, one address and import lead times, we would like to quote it.
  • Reinstatement of data R10,000 and increased cost of working R10,000 are low for a medical imaging business. Both can be increased.
  • Wreckage removal R10,000 is light for a Hino. We have asked for it lifted.
Our recommendation Place the full programme. Insuring eleven vehicles worth R3.89 million while leaving R73.3 million of stock and R75 million of annual carry uninsured is not a smaller version of the same programme — it is a different one, and it does not cover the way your business actually loses money. If budget is the constraint, tell us the number and we will build the programme around it rather than cut it at the vehicles.
