The finding that decides it

The tanker is insured as a truck, not as a tanker

Three holes, all on the face of the quote 1. Third party R2,500,000, but R1,000,000 for fire or explosion (Sub Section B limits, p12), "Increased third party liability: No" on all eleven. 2. Ask this before anything else

Is the tanker on the road today? It is not on the Santam vehicle list. If it runs, it is insured somewhere else (get that schedule) or not at all (then it is urgent, whoever wins). Same shape as the nine tracked Tshenolo vehicles that were not on its policy.

And look at the Santam item numbers: PC items 2, 4 and 11 are missing from the list (1, 3, 5 to 10, 12, 13). Three vehicles have come off this policy. Ask whether the tanker was ever one of them.

What VAPS will ask once it is declared
  • Own use or delivery? Diesel for the abattoir and their own fleet is a different risk from selling fuel to customers.
  • Drivers: PrDP endorsed for dangerous goods, each one.
  • Placards and the emergency information carried in the cab.
  • Where it parks and fills overnight.
  • Declare it as a dangerous goods carrier in writing so nobody can say later it was not disclosed.
The ask to VAPS, in one e-mail (1) Envirosure HAZ pollution liability on HH10SGGP, priced, with its excess reducer; (2) increased third party to R5,000,000 including fire and explosion; (3) tanker declared as a dangerous goods carrier; (4) business description corrected (Play 04); (5) confirm VAPS rated on the client's claims record. Expect the tanker to cost more than R3,082.58 once this is done. That is the honest number.

Play 05 · the red team · RM eyes only

The Naked Truth

DO NOT HAND
TO THE CLIENT

Six things the client or Bell Ryck can throw at you. Raise them first and you look like the expert. Every one has an honest answer.

1
"So why move at all?"
R26.44 is not a reason. If the client has no service complaint, Santam may just match the add-ons.
SAY"U skuif nie vir die prys nie. U kry roadside van R20,000 per trok, windscreen en u excess op elke voertuig afgekoop, vir dieselfde geld."
2
The Fuso is dearer
+R784.49 a month at VAPS, the biggest single swing in the file.
SAY"Ja, die Fuso kos meer. Daarvoor is sy excess R5,000 in plaas van minimum R30,000, en hy kry R20,000 roadside."
3
Santam is better on a small trailer claim
10% min R1,000 against a R5,000 inner. Level at R50,000; VAPS wins above it.
SAY"Op 'n klein skade betaal u by Santam minder. Op 'n groot een, of as die trailer afgeskryf word, betaal u by VAPS net R5,000."
4
We have not seen Santam's limits
Three pages of 72. If Santam's third party is R5m and ours is R2.5m, that is a regression we have not disclosed yet.
SAY"Ek wil nie iets vergelyk wat ek nie gesien het nie. Stuur vir my die volle skedule en ek wys u lyn vir lyn."
5
"Hire out service"
If vehicles are hired to third parties, VAPS has not rated that, and the business description says "Transporter".
SAY"Verhuur julle voertuie aan ander mense? Dit moet op die polis staan, anders kan hulle 'n eis weier."
6
The tanker price will go up
HAZ pollution and increased TP are not free. The +R3,056 is a floor, not the answer.
SAY"Die R3,082 vir die tanker is sonder spill cover. Ek gee u die regte syfer voor u iets teken."
7
Theft cover is conditional on four units
Tanker, Fuso, Hyundai, Hilux are over R200,000. No tracking with recovery, no theft or hijack claim. Every tracking field on the quote is blank.
SAY"VAPS betaal nie 'n diefstal eis op daai vier sonder tracking met recovery nie. Kom ons kry dit reg voor die eerste Oktober."
8
The quote has typing errors
"Transporter", MVBITNGP, a tanker from year 0, DJ6WDGP's year, Sasria on nine of eleven. Bell Ryck will use them to call the quote careless.
SAY"Ons het dit self gevang en laat regmaak. Dit is presies hoekom u 'n broker wil hê wat die skedule lees."
Before you present Have in hand: where the tanker is insured today, VAPS's written answer on HAZ and increased third party, and either the full Santam schedule or a clear line that says you have not seen it. Do not present the R11,485.94 as the price.
Bottom line This is a service move with a tanker problem attached. Win it on roadside, excess and one policy for the whole fleet. Do not let it become "VAPS is cheaper", because it is not, and do not let the client bind with the tanker as quoted.

What you do now

Your moves, in order

  • 1
    Ask where the tanker is insured today
    If nowhere and it is running, that is the first conversation, whoever writes the risk.
  • 2
    Get the full Santam schedule
    E-mail or Google Drive. Third party limits and the excess table decide whether the pack's "to be confirmed" lines turn into gains or regressions.
  • 3
    One e-mail to VAPS
    HAZ pollution + increased TP on the tanker; dangerous goods declared; business description; MV81TNGP; DJ6WDGP year; tanker year, owner, finance; Sasria on the Prado and SLK; claims record confirmed.
  • 4
    Ask Bell Ryck or Santam to price the tanker
    So the client sees eleven against eleven. If they will not, the pack already says the comparison is ten against ten.
  • 5
    Tracking on four units
    Tanker, Fuso, Hyundai, Hilux: over R200,000, so tracking with recovery or no theft cover at VAPS. The trailer has "Santam approved security: No". Cartrack lead, and a condition of the policy.
  • 6
    Ask about the abattoir
    No buildings, cold rooms, deterioration of stock, machinery breakdown, BI or liability on either document. If it is uninsured, it is a bigger account than the fleet.
