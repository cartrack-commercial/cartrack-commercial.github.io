How to run the meeting
1Open with the tracking condition, not the price. Their schedule says theft cover needs a monitored device on 20 vehicles and every item reads "Tracking device required: No". That is a live hole and it earns you the room.
2Walk the scoreboard. Let them see that the two cheapest-looking quotes cover goods in South Africa only — before they ask why you didn't lead with the cheapest.
3Land OWNsurance on cover, then price. R10m third party held, agreed value held, no excess. The R14,656 comes last.
4Say the fund out loud yourself. "Annual savings R341,670" is their own money, not a saving. If they discover that after you present, you lose the file.

The Naked Truth

DO NOT HAND
TO THE CLIENT

Six places OWNsurance is weaker or unproven. A transport client who has been quoted five times this month will find these. Raise every one before they do.

1
The quote is expressly subject to a satisfactory claims history — and the history is 96%.
Line one of the OWNsurance quotation. R2,402,455 paid in 24 months against R2,491,002 of motor premium.
SAY"I've asked them to confirm in writing that they've rated on your actual record before I put a number in front of you. I'm not going to quote you a price that moves."
2
The goods cover drops from 16 countries to 11.
Angola, Uganda, Rwanda and Kenya fall away. Zambia, Tanzania and DRC to Kolwezi are retained.
SAY"Tell me what you actually run. If Kenya and Angola are live routes I'll ask for them back before we bind — and if they're not, we're not paying for territory you don't use."
3
Twelve-month lock-in, and cancelling forfeits the fund.
Cancel inside the year and any surplus is forfeited; a negative balance is payable on demand.
SAY"It's an annual contract and I'd rather you hear that from me. You're committing for twelve months — which is why the three confirmations matter before you sign, not after."
4
Third party is R10m for general haulage — R5m for petrol and diesel, R2.5m for chemicals.
The fleet carries fuel trailers, DC tankers and a tri-tank. If petroleum moves, the headline limit is not the limit.
SAY"What goes in the tankers? I need that on record, because the liability limit follows the load and I'm not letting you find that out at a claim."
5
Public liability R20,000,000 and driver personal accident are not on the quote at all.
ONE carries both; OWNsurance quotes neither.
SAY"Two sections aren't on this quote and I'm not letting them lapse quietly. I'll place them alongside so your position is whole."
6
Sums insured only adjust at renewal, and there is a confidentiality clause.
No mid-term value corrections. The client may not disclose the product structure to another broker or underwriter.
SAY"Get your values right now, because they're fixed for twelve months. And yes — there's a confidentiality clause on how the product works. I'd rather point at it than have you find it."

The objections you'll hit — and the words that answer them
"MiWay is cheaper. Why aren't we taking that?"
Because it isn't the same policy. Your trucks move from agreed value to retail — a 2013 MAN settles at book, not at your figure. All your loads share one R2,000,000 limit instead of R2,000,000 a truck. The goods excess doubles to R100,000. Theft of a load by your own driver is excluded. And nothing is covered outside South Africa. That's not R10,000 cheaper, that's a different product.
"The OWNsurance quote says I save R341,670 a year. You said R175,878."
Both are true and they're different things. R175,878 is what you stop paying. R341,670 is your own money going into your own fund, which pays your own first claims — you get back whatever the claims haven't used. On your record I'd expect the fund to be working hard, so I'm selling you this on the excess, not the rebate.
"So what actually changes when I claim?"
Today you pay R10,000 on every motor claim and R50,000 on a load. On this you pay nothing on own damage, theft, hijack or third party, and R5,000 on a load. You had eight accidents in June alone — that's R80,000 of excess you wouldn't have paid.
"King Price came in about the same. Why not run that?"
It's R606 a month more than you pay now and it takes your third-party liability from R10 million to R2.5 million — R1 million if there's a fire. One serious accident on the N3 and that difference is the whole business. I'm not putting that in front of you as an option.
