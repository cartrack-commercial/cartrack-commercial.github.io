Something to fix this week, whoever insures you

Your policy requires tracking on the buses. The schedule says none is fitted.

This is the most urgent thing in this pack and it has nothing to do with changing insurer.

What your current schedule says, on every bus
Gearlock: No · Immobiliser: Factory Fitted · Tracking 1: Early Warning System Required · Drivecam: No · Data Dot: No · Alarm system: None
and under Warranties and Endorsements, on every item:
"Vehicle Recovery System (the Device) Warranty and Memorandum"
Why that wording matters An early warning system is required, and this is written as a warranty, not a recommendation. Your own schedule records no tracking device and no alarm on the buses, only a factory-fitted immobiliser. A breached warranty is the cleanest ground an insurer has to decline a claim. This applies to 24 of your 25 vehicles and it applies today, on the policy you already hold.
And every quote keeps the requirement Bryte prints "Early warning system required" against 21 of 25 items. King Price requires an immobiliser or tracking on every vehicle, tracking on anything worth R500,000 or more, tested every six months, and adds an extra excess of 25% of the claim where it is not there. Auto & General charges a theft excess of R5,600 or 7.5% of the vehicle value, whichever is higher, and waives it entirely if a tracking device is fitted, which on a R450,000 Quantum is about R33,750 of excess removed.
What we suggest Fit the units before any new policy incepts, so you are compliant on day one rather than finding out at a claim. On a fleet carrying special-needs children there is a second reason that has nothing to do with insurance: live vehicle location and driver behaviour reporting is what a parent and a governing body actually want to see. We can quote 24 units alongside whichever policy you choose.
