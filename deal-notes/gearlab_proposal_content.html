The section that matters most

Neither quote covers a car on your hoist

What the Hollard wording actually says "The company shall not be liable under this section in respect of loss, damage and/or injury occurring by or through the use of any vehicle hoist or vehicle ramp unless otherwise stated in the Schedule."
It is not stated. "Vehicle hoists and ramps" reads No on all four items. The same section also excludes "defective workmanship or consequential loss as a result thereof to any vehicle in or on the premises".
And what the ONE schedule says Car hoists are included, which is better. But the internal risk records "Damage to vehicles being worked upon: NO." That is the sentence that describes what you do for a living.
What we are doing about it We have gone back to both insurers to have those two extensions quoted, and we will not put a final recommendation in front of you until we have them. We are also asking you one question: what is the highest total value of customers' vehicles standing at your premises at any one time? R50,000 will not replace one modern SUV, and a workshop of this type usually has several cars in at once. That limit needs to be set from your yard, not from a template.

Four things to settle before either policy starts

Details that decide whether a claim pays

1 · The two quotations name different companies Hollard insures "Gear Lab", registration 2000/260824/07. ONE insures "Gear Lab Roodepoort (Pty) Ltd", registration 2025/509941/07 — one registered in 2000, the other in 2025, at the same address.
Insuring the wrong company means there is no cover when you claim. Please confirm which entity trades, employs the staff, and takes the customers' vehicles in. We will put that name on both. The VAT number on the Hollard quote also needs correcting — the figure shown is not a valid South African VAT number.
2 · The two insurers disagree about your building Hollard insures the building at R1,750,000. ONE insures it at R2,555,000. That is a R805,000 difference on the same premises, and only one of them can be right. Please confirm what it would cost to rebuild it today — if the figure is too low, a partial claim is reduced proportionally.
3 · Both insurers have asked for something you do not have ONE's approval makes the start of cover conditional on receiving a satisfactory claims history and your previous insurance policy schedule. You have not been insured, so neither exists. That is not a problem in itself — but an insurer prices an uninsured business differently from one changing insurers, and we would rather they confirm the price knowing that now than revisit it after you have signed. We are putting it to them in writing.
4 · What neither quotation includes Worth knowing, so the decision is yours rather than an accident of what was quoted: no business interruption (if the workshop burns, the income stops and the salaries and rent do not) · no machinery breakdown on your hoists, presses and benches · no electronic equipment cover for diagnostic gear · no money, glass, fidelity or goods in transit · and no cover for any vehicle Gear Lab owns. Tell us whether you run a bakkie or a recovery vehicle and we will add it.
Of those, business interruption is the one we would most like to quote — you carry R5,200,000 of stock at a single address.
