Play 04
The biggest risk in the file is not a truck

Products liability, recall, and section 61

Consumer Protection Act, section 61: the producer, importer, distributor and retailer are each liable for harm caused by unsafe goods, without proof of negligence. This group is all four: Bandini Cheese and Fresco produce, Impact imports and distributes, Elena's sells.

Ask the client, then BryteWhy
Who carries products liability?
Limit per event and aggregate; all five companies named; imported ranges included.
Any manufacturing exclusion?
CS Continental's OUTsurance wording excluded harm from goods the insured "prepared and packaged". A cheese maker hits that head-on.
Claims-made? Retro date?
If claims-made, the retro date must carry across or past production is uninsured.
Product recall / contamination?
Usually a separate policy. Most SA food SMEs do not have it.
Pollution?
Dairy effluent at Doornkop; ammonia if the cold rooms use it.
Who insures the property?
Factory, warehouse, bottling plant, stock, BI. Might be Bryte under COM208596JB, might be elsewhere.
In the room "As een batch mozzarella besmet is en dit gaan na honderd pizzerias, is julle aanspreeklik, al het niemand 'n fout gemaak nie. Dis die Consumer Protection Act. Wie dek julle vandag daarvoor, en vir hoeveel? Want dit is 'n groter risiko as enige trok."
The precedent, use it carefully

The 2018 listeriosis outbreak, traced to a processed-meat plant, is the example every food business in SA knows. Do not dramatise it; just ask what would happen if it were their product.

How to price it

Products liability usually sits in a broadform liability section rated on turnover. We will need group turnover by company, export sales if any, and product types. Recall needs a separate submission: recall plan, batch traceability, largest customer.

Liability and recall submission: what the market will ask for
Turnover by company, last and next year
Export sales, if any (Namibia, Botswana?)
Product list and imported brands
Food safety certification (HACCP, FSSC 22000)
Recall plan and batch traceability
Largest customers and contracts
Liability claims in 5 years
Current liability limits and retro date
Also on the needs list (proposal page 3) GIT with deterioration in transit and driver dishonesty · deterioration of stock in cold rooms (check for a load-shedding exclusion, as on Vuyos) · machinery breakdown on pasteurisers, boilers and the bottling line · BI (one cheese factory feeds the whole distributor) · fidelity and money · accounts receivable or trade credit (they sell on account to restaurants) · cyber (online ordering, POPIA) · GPA for drivers.

Play 06 · the red team · RM eyes only

The Naked Truth

DO NOT HAND
TO THE CLIENT

Where this pack is weak. Raise them first.

1
"Both quotes are dearer than Bryte"
As quoted, yes: VAPS +R26,981, Hollard +R19,596. But Bryte's increase is unknown, and the extras (roadside, windscreen, fees) explain most of it.
SAY"Op die voertuie alleen is VAPS R6,489 goedkoper as Bryte, en Bryte se increase kom nog. Die res is ekstras wat ons kan uithaal."
2
We do not know Bryte's increase
The comparison is against a renewal that has not been priced yet.
SAY"Bryte het nog nie gesê hoeveel die increase is nie. Ek vra dit vandag, dan wys ek die regte verskil."
3
Two different fleet counts
Bryte 159 units, VAPS 155. Trucks R1.87m higher at Bryte. The fleet list settles it.
SAY"Ek wil elke voertuig op die lys sien, met sy waarde. Dan weet ons albei kwotasies dek dieselfde."
4
"We have products liability"
They may. We have not seen it. Do not imply they are uninsured.
SAY"Goed, stuur vir my die skedule. Ek wil net seker maak al vyf maatskappye en julle invoere is daarop."
5
The claims period
Only "2026" is written on the declaration. If R3.84m is one year, both prices move.
SAY"Watter jare is dit? Dit bepaal alles: die prys, die excess en of die aggregate opsie sin maak."
6
Tracking cost lands on them
Up to 112 units plus video telematics.
SAY"Met diefstal as die helfte van julle claims, sal enige versekeraar dit vra. Dis wat julle eis laat betaal."
7
Bryte's aggregate looks cheaper
R1.97m insurer premium against R2.32m, but the client funds up to R2.5m of truck claims and pays 90% up front.
SAY"Die laer premie is omdat julle self die eerste R2.5m se trok-eise betaal. Met julle claims history moet ons dit eers uitwerk."
8
Hollard's excess on the cars
5% of the vehicle's value: R23,921 on an average SUV for any claim. A R20,000 dent pays nothing.
SAY"Hollard se cover is sterker, maar op die karre is die excess baie hoër. Ons laat die excess reducers prys voor julle kies."
Bottom line The fleet is close on price; the account is wide open. Win the review by showing them what nobody has: which company carries products liability, whether there is recall cover, and whether their loads, cold rooms and factory are insured. Then the fleet follows.
