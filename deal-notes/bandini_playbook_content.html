Play 02
The decisive cover for a distributor

The trucks are insured. The cheese is not.

On the face of the quote Goods in Transit: Not taken. GIT excess reducers not taken. GIT 24/7 Assist not taken. The VAPS proposal form section 5 (commodities, load limit per truck) is blank. With R1,876,816 of theft in their claims, the hijacked load is the loss they are most likely to have.
Ask first
  • Is GIT on another policy today? If yes, get that schedule too; the comparison is incomplete without it.
  • What is a full load worth? Cheese per truck, highest single load.
  • Refrigerated? If so, ask for deterioration of stock / refrigeration breakdown on GIT. That is the characteristic loss for a food distributor (same instinct as CS Continental).
  • Own goods only, or also other people's? Changes the GIT wording and the business description.
The VAPS rules that come with GIT
  • GIT load limit R1,200,000 or more: independent tracking units on both trailer links, wired, 5-day battery backup, trailer disconnect alerts.
  • Theft and hijack on GIT only pays with the approved tracking in place, same as the vehicles.
  • VAPS wants a full description of goods. "General goods" is not accepted.

The loss ratio, if it is 3 years

R3.84m over 3 years is R1.28m a year against R2.64m of VAPS premium, about 49%. Workable, which is probably why VAPS quoted the whole fleet. But we do not know the period, and the price is "subject to 3 years written claims". If the written record is worse, the rate moves. Get the dates from the client before you present a number as firm.

Theft is 49% of everything

VAPS will not pay a theft or hijack claim on anything over R200,000 without approved tracking with recovery, working, paid, and monitored 24 hours. On the group averages that is 42 trucks, 32 trailers and 19 SUVs = 93 units, more once the special types and bus are counted (up to about 112). Trucks over R800,000 on medium or long haul also need two-camera video telematics.

Five questions about the claims
  • Which years? Everything about the price depends on the period.
  • How many theft claims make up R1,876,816, and were the loads stolen too?
  • How much of the R1,711,448 was actually recovered from third parties?
  • The trailer roof, R170,602: low bridge or depot? A driver training point either way.
  • Any GIT, liability or property claims on other policies we have not seen?
The Cartrack play, and it is real This is a 93 to 112 unit lead where tracking is a condition precedent, plus video telematics on the bigger trucks and trailer units on 32 trailers. Find out what is fitted today, by whom, and whether it is monitored. The theft history says whatever is there is not working well enough.
In the room "Amper die helfte van julle claims is diefstal. VAPS betaal nie 'n diefstal eis op 'n voertuig bo R200,000 sonder 'n werkende tracker met recovery nie. Kom ons kyk eers wat op die trokke en trailers is, voordat ons enigiets teken."

Ask 1: hail on vehicles parked in the open

Page 18, the Renasa commercial excess table, under "Motor Dealerships, Importers, Fleet owners": hail excess R1,500 under carports or nets, and "Vehicles in the open not under carports or hail nets: not covered". It sits in a generic table "applicable if not otherwise stated" and is probably aimed at motor traders' stock, but 155 vehicles in a Robertsham yard in a Gauteng summer is exactly where it would bite. Get it in writing that it does not apply to the Motor Fleet section.

Ask 2: the night loading

HCV table: +5% of the claim between 23h00 and 04h00. Food distribution trucks often leave before dawn. Find out the shift pattern; if they run at night, ask for the penalty excess reducer (it is on the proposal form, section 7). Same question as Tshenolo and Cordiguard.

Also: capsizing whilst tipping adds 5% of the vehicle's value, only relevant if any truck tips.

Up to about 112 units, plus video telematics. They may see it as a cost of moving.

"Transport Contractor"
The quote misdescribes them. If we had not caught it, a claim could have been challenged.
SAY"Ons het dit self gevang en laat regmaak. Dis presies hoekom julle 'n broker wil hê wat die skedule lees."

"Who is Renasa?"
VAPS is the underwriting manager; Renasa (Telesure group, FSP 15491) carries the risk. Clients sometimes only know the big names.

19 SUVs on a business policy
R11.6m of SUVs rated at the same 3.75% as the trucks. If they are directors' cars, a private policy may be cheaper.
SAY"Wie ry die SUVs? As dit die direkteure s'n is, kyk ek of 'n private polis hulle beter pas."
Before you present Have in hand: the current schedule or at least the debit order and insurer, where GIT sits today, the claims dates, and what tracking is fitted. Do not present R219,941.79 as a saving.
Bottom line This is a review sale, not a price sale. We found the missing load cover, the missing agreed values, the wrong business description and a theft record that makes tracking a condition. That is worth more to them than a number we cannot yet prove.
