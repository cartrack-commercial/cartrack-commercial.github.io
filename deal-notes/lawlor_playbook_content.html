⏰ Fix 3 — 14 days, or no theft cover Hollard requires early-warning trackers on ALL vehicles within 14 days of inception. It is a condition of cover. Miss the window and theft and hijack fall away on a fleet that includes a R5m Porsche and a R2m Charger.

This is the easiest telematics conversation in the book — don't turn it into a pitch, just book the fitment.
