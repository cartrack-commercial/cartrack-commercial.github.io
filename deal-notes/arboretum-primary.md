## 🚨 FINDING 1: Hollard has excluded the things a school actually gets sued for

Endorsement **LIA_011 "Schools exception"** on the Combined Liability section removes indemnity
for any claim arising out of:

1. **child molestation or sexual abuse of any kind**
2. **wrongful or excessive discipline of any kind**
3. **bullying or harassment of any kind**

and the section memo adds **"Pupil-to-Pupil Liability is excluded"**.

Those four categories are close to the whole liability exposure of a primary school. Alongside
that, Hollard's combined liability is **R1,000,000** (claims-made, **retroactive date 01 Oct 2026,
i.e. inception**, so every prior year of the school's operation goes uninsured), with **no**
statutory legal defence costs, **no** wrongful arrest and defamation, and **no** products or
defective workmanship.

**Against the current Santam programme that is not a cheaper policy, it is a different and much
smaller one.** Bench it.

⚠️ And the umbrella probably cannot attach. Hollard quotes **Umbrella Liability R17,500,000**, but
its own Schedule of Liability Limits requires an **underlying limit of R2,500,000** on General,
Tenants and Property Owner's liability. The underlying Combined Liability is **R1,000,000**.
Same shape as the Powerflow/Western umbrella find. This morning's "correction" to R1m may have
created the mismatch.

Two things on the current policy that nobody has costed:
