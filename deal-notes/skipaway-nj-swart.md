## 🚨 THE FINDING: the tanker is not insured for what it does

1. **Third party is R2,500,000, but only R1,000,000 if the accident involves fire or explosion**
   (Sub Section B limits, p12). "Increased third party liability: No" on all 11 items. On a truck
   carrying 8,000 L of diesel, **fire is the loss**. Same cap as CS Continental and Twin Trans.
2. **The spillage extension excludes the load.** "Fuel Tank Spillage and Leakage" R250,000 (R10,000
   first amount) covers the vehicle's **own** fuel tank and engine, and *"does not provide cover for
   any costs and expenses associated with the spillage of Dangerous Goods as identified and
   classified by the code of practice SABS 0228"*. **Diesel is a Class 3 dangerous good.** An 8,000 L
   load spill is not covered.
3. **Pollution Liability (Envirosure HAZ): Not taken.** The cover built for exactly this is on the
   quote, unticked. So is its excess reducer.
4. **Is the tanker on the road today?** If the client already runs it, it is not on the Santam
   schedule, so **it is either insured somewhere else or not insured at all.** Ask before anything
   else. Same shape as the 9 vehicles on the Tshenolo tracking list that were not on its policy.

**Fix before binding:** price Envirosure HAZ pollution liability and increased third party on the
tanker, and declare it to VAPS as a dangerous-goods carrier (driver with a dangerous-goods PrDP,
placarding). The tanker's own fields are also blank: model year printed as **"0"**, VESA approved
tracking, HP institution and registered owner all empty.

## Other findings

- ⚠️ **Wrong business description.** VAPS: **"Transporter"**. Santam: **"Abattoir with cold storage;
  Hire out service"**. A misdescribed risk is a non-disclosure problem at claim stage. And **if any
  vehicle is hired out to third parties** (Santam records "Business: Hire out service" on the
  trailer), VAPS must be told.
- ⚠️ **A registration keyed wrong.** VAPS prints the Hyundai H100 as **`MVBITNGP`**, which is not a
  valid plate. Santam's schedule font draws "1" like a capital I (its prices read "RI,393.43"), so
  Santam's "MV8ITNGP" is **MV81TNGP**. VAPS keyed 8 as B and 1 as I off the Santam schedule. Correct it.
- ⚠️ **A model year differs.** Daihatsu Gran Max **DJ6WDGP**: Santam **2014**, VAPS **2019**, same
  value R76,900. Also check the plate itself: both documents print one digit where a Gauteng plate
  has two.
- **Excess, visible for one vehicle only.** Trailer JT61KMGP: Santam **10% of claim, minimum R1,000**
  (theft/hijack 10%, minimum R1,000; glass 25%, minimum R350). VAPS base on the same trailer is 10%,
  minimum R15,000, cut to an **R5,000 inner excess** only by the R150/mo reducer. Santam is better on
  the one item we can see. VAPS HCV base above R200,000 is 10% of claim **minimum R30,000**, and
  without the reducers the whole comparison changes. Need the rest of the Santam schedule.
- **Clean record.** Santam shows **claim free group 7** on the trailer. VAPS prints no
  claims-experience condition, so the price may be firm, but confirm VAPS rated on the record.
- 🛰️ **Tracking.** VAPS requires tracking with recovery on every vehicle **over R200,000**: the
  tanker (R500,000), Fuso CZ74MNGP (R350,000), Hyundai (R241,700), Hilux MZ98SBGP (R237,000). **Four
  units, tracking fields blank.** Santam records "approved factory fitted security system: No" on the
  trailer. Cartrack lead, and a theft-cover condition.
- ❓ **The abattoir itself.** Neither document carries property, deterioration of stock, machinery
  breakdown, business interruption or public/products liability. **A cold-storage abattoir with no
  deterioration of stock or machinery breakdown cover** is the characteristic loss uninsured. Ask
  whether it is insured elsewhere. If not, it is a bigger opportunity than the fleet.

## Recommendation

**Price is not a reason to move: on the same ten vehicles it is R26.44 a month.** If the client wants
to move for service, VAPS works, **but not with the tanker as quoted.** Fix the tanker's cover first,
get Santam's price for the tanker so the comparison is 11 against 11, correct the business
description and the two vehicle errors, then present.
