01
The one section that decides this renewal

Who counts as a passenger, and who pays a fare

Passenger liability is the cover that answers when someone on one of your buses is injured or killed. For an operator carrying schoolchildren and commuters it is the most important line in the policy. Two separate questions sit on it — and the underwriter's 5 August reply answers one and closes the door on the other.

Question 1 — the wording · still open

They confirm the section covers fare-paying passengers. But the schedule, now unchanged through four revisions, still reads:

  • cover for "death of or bodily injury to e-hailing fare paying passenger"
  • "The Passengers must be… in a vehicle which is active on an E-hailing platform"
  • excluded: "any incident… while the vehicle is in use for a non-e-hailing platform"
Why an email does not settle it At a claim, the adjuster reads the schedule. An assurance in correspondence carries far less weight than the printed section — and none at all if the underwriter who wrote it has moved on. We have asked for the e-hailing references to be removed from the schedule, not merely explained.
Question 2 — non-fare-paying · now answered

The same reply confirms King Price cannot endorse non-fare-paying passenger liability. Santam covers it today at R2,500,000. On a bus operation, that reaches further than it first appears:

  • WCED scholar transport — the department pays, not the child in the seat
  • Charter work — the fare is paid by the organiser, not the passengers
  • Conductors and assistants travelling on duty
  • Anyone carried without charge, for whatever reason
Disclosed to you in writing This is a reduction against your current cover. We are obliged to put it in front of you before you decide, and it forms part of our record of advice.
Where that leaves the decision A limit that does not respond is worth less than no limit at all, because it creates the belief that you are covered. Until King Price issue a schedule that describes the buses you actually run — and until the position on passengers who do not personally pay a fare is resolved — the R3,070 a month is not a saving we can ask you to take.
