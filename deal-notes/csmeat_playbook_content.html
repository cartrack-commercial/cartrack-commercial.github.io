The Naked Truth

DO NOT HAND
TO THE CLIENT

Five things that can cost you this deal. The first one is not a cover point — it is your price.

1
The price may not survive underwriting.
19 motor fleet incidents, ≈R1.46m paid over ten years, R968,238 in the last 24 months against ≈R645,000 of premium. That is a loss ratio near 150%. Nothing on VAPS52226 says "subject to claims experience" — which is exactly why you must ask. Do not send R20,981.44 to the client until VAPS confirm in writing that they rated on that record.
DOEmail VAPS today: "Please confirm this quotation is rated on the attached ten-year claims experience, and advise any loading."
2
Third party liability halves — and drops to R1m on fire or explosion.
OUTsurance gives R5,000,000 per vehicle. VAPS Sub-Section B is R2,500,000 any one event, R1,000,000 where the accident is due to fire or explosion. On a nine-vehicle delivery fleet in Gauteng that is thin, and it is the one cover point a sharp client will find.
SAY"I am not putting this in front of you at two and a half million. I have asked for it back at five, and the fire limit lifted with it."
3
No theft cover without proven tracking.
VAPS requirement effective 1 March 2023: every vehicle over R200,000 needs a tracking device with a recovery service — on the values printed, that is six of the nine. The other three need VESA level 3/4 or a factory alarm/immobiliser. The tracking fields on all nine items are blank. No proof of fitment, operation, paid subscription and 24-hour manned monitoring means no theft or hijack payout at all.
SAY"OUTsurance already names Cartrack on its approved list. Let me audit what is on each vehicle and close the gap before we bind — not after a hijack."
4
Credit shortfall, medical expenses and claims preparation all shrink.
Credit shortfall gone on both financed Hiluxes (OUTsurance charges R507.84/mo for it). Claims preparation R10,000 → R5,000. Medical expenses R5,000 per vehicle is on the OUTsurance schedule and absent from the King Price quote — and King Price's own note says an unstated cover is no cover. Windscreen capped at R15,000 with the excess waiver not taken.
DOPrice all four back. Even if they cost R1,500/mo together, the saving is still roughly R4,400/mo — but get the real figures, do not estimate to the client.
5
The inception date has already passed and their identity is wrong.
VAPS52226 is signed 04/08/2026 with inception 01/08/2026. OUTsurance renewed on 01/08/2026 — four days ago. And the quote carries OUTsurance's own company registration and VAT numbers in the policyholder fields.
DOReset the inception date, correct the registration and VAT numbers, and time the OUTsurance cancellation to the day. No gap, no double debit.

The objections you'll hit — and the words that answer them
"OUTsurance has been good to us for ten years."
They have paid you well — nearly one and a half million over that time. That is also why your premium is where it is, and why your own schedule says you no longer qualify for an OUTbonus. Loyalty has already been priced in. What I can change is what you pay each month and what you carry on every claim.
"Your third party cover is half of what we have."
It is, on the quote as issued — and I raised it before you did. I have asked King Price to put it back to five million, including the fire limit. I am not going to move you onto a thinner liability limit to win a premium argument.
"We are with an OUTsurance broker already, why do we need you?"
Lara is an OUTsurance broker — she can only show you OUTsurance. I can take you to the whole market, and the first place I took you came back nearly six thousand a month cheaper on the same nine vehicles.
"How do I know this price will hold?"
Because I am not giving it to you until the underwriter confirms it in writing against your claims record. You will get one number, and it will be the number you pay.
