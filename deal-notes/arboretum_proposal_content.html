04
Four things worth fixing · whoever you stay with

Found while reading your schedule

Some of what we found is not about moving insurer at all. It is about gaps that have been sitting in your programme for years, and one piece of cover you are about to lose without anybody mentioning it.

  • 1
    About ten million rand of automatic uplift is not in any quote
    Your Santam policy carries escalation and inflation as scheduled amounts: R4,043,668 and R4,674,761 on the Waterberry Wood buildings, R587,528 and R646,281 on Wag 'n Bietjie. That is R9,952,238 that rises with rebuilding costs. None of the three quotes carries an escalation or inflation line. We are asking all three to price it.
  • 2
    There is no theft, fidelity or business interruption cover anywhere
    Not on your current policy, and not on any of the three quotes. Fidelity is the one we would push. A school collects fees, runs a tuck shop and holds fundraisers, and employee dishonesty is the characteristic loss of the trade. Nobody has ever put it in front of you.
  • 3
    Glass at R10,000 and accidental damage at R20,000
    These are on your current policy, so they are long-standing rather than something a quote got wrong. R10,000 would not reglaze one classroom block, and R20,000 of accidental damage sits against a property programme of more than fifty million. Both are inexpensive to lift.
  • 4
    Two conditions that could cost you a claim
    ONE's liability cover is conditional on disclaimer notice boards being displayed at the entrances. Your electronic equipment cover, on every quote, carries a reduced excess only if the main distribution board has certified SANS 10142-1 surge protection. Both are an afternoon's work and both are worth confirming.
One for your governing body Your Santam schedule records that Government is a co-insurer for the balance of the Waterberry Wood buildings. None of the three quotes reflects that arrangement. Before you move, we want it stated on the new schedule, so that the split of who insures what is not left to be argued after a fire.
