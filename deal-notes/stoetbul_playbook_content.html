The Naked Truth

DO NOT HAND
TO THE CLIENT

Five things a sharp client will find. The first one is a correction to your own brief.

1
Cash cover is not a shortcoming. It looks like a gain.
You flagged "kontant cover" as a weak point. On the schedules: Santam R50,000 — but carved into sub-limits, only R1,500 on the premises outside trading hours and R25,000 in a director's home. Western R50,000 major limit, plus receptacles R10,000, crossed cheques R150,000 and assault benefits. Check the out-of-hours wording before you concede it.
DODon't hand them a weakness the documents don't show. If anything, money is a point in our favour.
2
The quote is indicative, not firm.
Page 1: "acceptance of this risk and the rating applied are subject to a satisfactory three-year claims history and satisfactory loss ratio. Written confirmation from the previous insurer must be received prior to inception." If they have claimed, this price moves.
DOGet the three-year record from Efficient Insure and OUTsurance before R22,136.51 goes in writing to the client.
3
Products liability drops R4.5m to R2.5m.
The only genuine reduction in the whole quote, and it is on a food business. Food and drink liability sits separately at R2.5m and is named on the schedule — which Santam's is not — but the headline products limit is still halved.
SAY"One thing comes down and I've already asked them to fix it — products liability. Food and drink is separately covered at two and a half million either way."
4
Santam thinks it is insuring a restaurant.
Santam's schedule: "Restaurant". Western's: "restaurant, pub and live music venue". If they run live music and a pub, the current insurer may not have been told. That is a disclosure problem on the policy they hold today — and an argument for moving.
ASK"Does Santam know about the live music side?" Ask it as a question, never as an accusation. If the answer is no, you have just found something worth more than the saving.
5
Our fee nearly doubled, and we added cover nobody asked for.
Fees R1,103.31 → R1,978.24. And R504.17 of buildings on a residence at 46 Mopani Road plus R760 car hire — neither on the current programme. Both are defensible; both look bad if the client finds them first.
SAY"My fee is higher and I'll show you where. I've also quoted your house and car hire — take them out and you save six thousand two a month instead of four nine."
