⚠ Why this decides it Non-disclosure can void the policy or a claim (AC&E Annexure A, p3). A passenger injured on a Bolt trip in a car rated "private and business" is exactly the claim that gets repudiated. Same shape as Wes-Kaap and Gateway, where the passenger cover was not what it looked like. No AC&E price goes to the client as firm until AC&E has it in writing.
And RTU is not clean either The drivers rent from SAI; they are not employees. RTU's own clause takes away cover when the car is rented out and not in the control of the insured or "a driver employed by the insured". Ask RTUSA in writing whether that has ever been applied to this client and how they read it. If neither insurer covers a renter who disappears with a car, it goes in the Record of Advice. The answer is the tracker's recovery service, which is our product.
