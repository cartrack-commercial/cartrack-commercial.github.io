The most urgent item on your policy

A R100,000 excess until the survey is cleared

Old Mutual surveyed 13/15 and 16 Rising Sun Street and listed items still to be done. Until they are, the policy applies a punitive excess of 10% of the claim, minimum R100,000, to damage at both premises. On a R1,000,000 fire claim you would carry R100,000 instead of R25,000.

Cover a printing business usually needs

What is not on your policy

Your policy pays to replace a damaged press or burnt stock. It does not pay for what happens to the business while that press is down. These are the gaps we will ask the market to price.

CoverTodayWhy it matters to you
Business interruption
not coveredA fire at 16 Rising Sun Street (R93.7m of plant and stock) would stop production. Salaries, rent and lost profit until you are printing again are not insured.
Machinery breakdown
not coveredA press that breaks down internally (motor, gearbox, electronics) is not a fire or accident claim. Repairs and the downtime fall on you.
Power surge on plant
not selectedPower surge is switched off on the fire section. Load-shedding and grid failure damage is excluded on the whole policy.
Buildings
not on this policyThe premises are described, but no building sum insured is shown. If you own any of them, they need cover.
Electronic equipment
not coveredPre-press computers, plate-setters and design equipment have no specialist cover.
Goods in transit
not coveredFinished print delivered in your own trucks is not insured on the road.
Money and fidelity
not coveredNo cover for cash, or for theft by staff.
Bursting pipes and geysers
not selectedWater damage from burst pipes onto paper stock is switched off.
Vehicle third party
R2,500,000One limit for all third party and passenger claims together. Heavy vehicles warrant R5,000,000.
After a fire, your policy today pays for
  • Replacing the damaged presses and plant
  • Replacing the burnt stock and paper
  • Office contents at that premises
And does not pay for
  • Salaries while nothing is printed
  • Rent and fixed costs during the rebuild
  • Profit on orders you cannot fill
  • The extra cost of outsourcing print to keep customers
What we will ask for A quotation for the cover you have today, like for like, and the same quotation with business interruption, machinery breakdown and power surge added, so you can see what each costs before you decide. Your liability retroactive date of 1 July 2003 must be carried over by any new insurer.

Tracking and theft cover

A commercial vehicle stolen or hijacked without a tracker carries an extra R5,000 to R10,000 excess, and a car over R750,000 a further 2.5% of the loss. Nine vehicles have a tracker fitted; the Mercedes S350, Defender, Fiat, Dyna, Hino 300 and forklift do not. Any new insurer will set its own tracking conditions; we will confirm them before you sign.
