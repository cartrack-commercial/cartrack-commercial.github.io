How the excess reducer works King Price's standard excess on a truck is 10% of the claim, minimum R30,000. The three reducers bought on this quotation (R2,658.72 a month together) bring what you actually pay down to a flat R5,000 — R2,500 on the Nissan and the Yaris. You settle the excess and King Price refunds the amount above that inner figure. On the R628,915 accident of January this year, that difference is what would have stayed in your account.

03
The honest ledger · what you gain and what you lose

Nothing here is kept from you

What King Price adds
  • Unauthorised passenger liability R2,500,000. Not on your policy today — OUTsurance offers it, it has simply not been taken.
  • Contingent liability R2,500,000. Answers when an employee uses their own vehicle on your business.
  • Fuel tank spillage and leakage R250,000. Clean-up after an accident.
  • Wreckage removal R50,000 and towing and salvage R100,000.
  • Fire extinguishing charges R35,000 and temporary repairs R35,000.
  • Loss of keys R50,000.
  • Movement of third party vehicles R1,000,000 — relevant on a yard where other people's vehicles stand.
  • Cross-border cover into Namibia, Botswana, Eswatini, Lesotho, Zimbabwe, Mozambique and Malawi.
  • A real roadside product: R20,000 a year on each truck, accident management, and a police or runner vehicle sent to guard the vehicle and its load. Today you have two call-outs a year.
What you would lose — and we will not accept
  • Third party liability halves, R5,000,000 to R2,500,000 — and drops to R1,000,000 where the accident involves fire or explosion. On a delivery fleet this is the most important number on the schedule. It must go back to R5m.
  • Credit shortfall falls away on the two 2026 Hiluxes, both financed through Capitec. If either were written off in the next two years you would carry the gap yourself.
  • Claims preparation costs R10,000 → R5,000.
  • Medical expenses R5,000 a vehicle is printed on your OUTsurance schedule and does not appear on the King Price quotation. King Price's own note says that where a cover is not stated, there is no cover. To be confirmed.
  • Windscreen is capped at R15,000 a vehicle and the excess waiver has not been taken. A truck screen above that figure is for your account.
The condition that matters most King Price will not pay a theft or hijack claim unless the vehicle had an approved tracking unit fitted and working, on a paid subscription, monitored 24 hours a day by a manned control room. On the values shown, six of your nine vehicles need tracking with a recovery service. The quotation records nothing in its tracking fields. This has to be evidenced vehicle by vehicle before cover starts.
