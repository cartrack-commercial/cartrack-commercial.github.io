## A · Must be resolved before we can present

**1 · The sprinkler waiver — please put this to your underwriter now.**
The client's current fire section records: *"SURVEY DONE — Stacking Heights exceeds 3m. sprinkler
system waived."* Your quotation is subject to a satisfactory survey.

The stock in trade is **R73,266,429** at a single address. **If your surveyor will not grant the
same waiver, the client is being asked to sprinkler a warehouse** — that is a capital project, not
a premium adjustment, and it would end the discussion. We are sending the existing survey and
waiver separately. **Please confirm in writing whether the waiver will be carried across, before we
present anything.**

Please also confirm your position on the current fire condition that **all fire hose reels be
pressure-tested annually by an accredited SAQCC-registered technician**.

**2 · The motor section states no excess at all.**
There is not a single first amount payable printed on either quotation. The current policy carries:

- Basic **R4,000** (three items) and **R5,000** (three items)
- Theft and hijack **10% of claim, minimum R5,000**
- Driver under 26 — additional 5%, minimum R2,500 · licence held two years or less — additional R2,000
- Windscreen 20% minimum R350 · locks and keys R250

The motor section falls from R18,168.58 to R12,163.43 a month — **33% — while carrying R404,885
more sum insured.** We need the full excess table in writing to be satisfied that the saving is not
sitting in the excess.

**3 · Passenger and unauthorised passenger liability drop from R5,000,000 to R2,500,000.**
The current policy carries **R5,000,000** for both, on eight of the eleven items. **Please quote to
restore R5,000,000** and show the additional premium so the client can decide.

While on that section: the current policy carves out a **fire / explosion sub-limit of R1,000,000**
within the third-party limit. Your quotation does not state one. **Please confirm whether the full
R5,000,000 responds to a fire or explosion loss** — if it does, that is an improvement we would
like to record.

**4 · The retroactive date on the products liability and defective workmanship cover.**
The combined liability block is headed **"RETROACTIVE DATE: INCEPTION DATE OF POLICY"** and then
states three lines down that **"retroactive date 01 June 2017 will be retained"**. The CIB schedule
confirms the client's retroactive date is **01/06/2017**, so we take the header to be a printing
error. **Please correct the schedule before inception.**

Separately, and importantly: the **products liability and defective workmanship R5,000,000 is new
cover.** **Please confirm in writing whether that section also carries the 01/06/2017 retroactive
date, or whether it runs from inception.** This client installs and repairs x-ray machines at
customer sites; if the defective workmanship cover starts at inception, none of their completed
installations is covered and the client must be told so in writing before they decide.
