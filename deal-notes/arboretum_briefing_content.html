🏆 What is good, and what worries me

The three buses, and five things to query

Play 03
✅ The best thing in this quote, and you should lead with it The school runs three buses: a 2013 Iveco 50C15V15 midibus and two Toyota Quantum 14 seaters. On the ONE quote each one carries passenger liability of R5,000,000, under a bus and coach specific extension, plus third party liability of R5,000,000.

That is better than two other passenger operators we have reviewed this year, both of whom sat at R2,500,000. On a vehicle carrying other people's children to school, this is the single most important line in the policy, and ONE has it right. Check what the current Santam policy gives on the same three buses, because that is where this deal will be won or lost.
🚨 Query 1 · Glass at R10,000 A primary school has a great deal of glass and children in it. R10,000 would not replace the windows on one classroom block. This looks like a placeholder rather than a considered figure. Ask what the real replacement value is.
🚨 Query 2 · Printers at R1,699,778 Printers are insured for almost as much as every desktop, laptop and piece of software in the school combined (R1,885,100). Possible, but improbable. Get the asset register behind that line, because if it is mislabelled the electronic equipment section is mis rated, and it is the largest premium line on the quote.
🚨 Query 3 · Money at R50,000 A school collects fees, runs tuck shops and holds fundraising events. R50,000 is thin, and there is no fidelity cover at all (see below). Ask what cash is actually held and moved.
🚨 Query 4 · Accidental damage at R20,000 Against a property programme of R59.6 million. Ask for it to be lifted, it is inexpensive cover.
🚨 Query 5 · Three sections are missing from BOTH policies No theft. No fidelity. No business interruption. They appear in ONE's generic excess schedule but carry no sum insured and no premium, so they are not taken. The current Santam policy does not have them either, which I could confirm from the one page of the scan that survived.
So these are standing gaps rather than regressions, and that is the right way to raise them. Fidelity is the one I would push: a school handles fees, and employee dishonesty is the characteristic loss of the trade. Nobody has ever put it to them.

Eight questions, and who to ask

Play 04
To ONE, before anything is presented
1 · The insured is wrong, and this is the most important item on the page
The current Santam policy names three parties: Arboretum Primary School, Arboretum Pre-Primary School and FTRRI. The ONE quote names only Arboretum Primary School. A claim by an entity that is not a named insured does not get paid. Get all three named in writing before this goes anywhere near the school.
2 · There is no inception date
Inception and endorsement both read 01/01/3000 and payment reads TBA. That is a system placeholder. We saw exactly the same thing on the ONE quote for Gear Lab, so it appears to be how ONE issues an unaccepted quote, but it still means no start date is set. Also note the quote says renewal 01/08/2027 while the current policy anniversary is 01/04/2027. Confirm which applies and time the cancellation so there is no gap and no double debit.
3 · Overnight parking, within 21 days
The motor section carries a condition that overnight parking arrangements must be disclosed within 21 days. On three buses parked at a school overnight, this is not a formality. Get it declared and accepted in writing rather than discovering it at a claim.
4 · The surge protection condition
The power surge excess is 10% of claim with a minimum of R2,000 only if the main distribution board is protected by surge protectors installed by a qualified electrician and certified compliant with SANS 10142-1. With R4.28 million of electronic equipment and R59.6 million of property, find out whether that certificate exists. If it does not, an electrician's morning fixes it. Endorsements SANS 0007 and SANS 0018 attach to this.
5 · Survey endorsements
The quote carries SURV 0006 and SURV 0016. Ask what the surveyor will require and whether the quote is conditional on a satisfactory survey, so a requirement does not land after the school has agreed to move.
6 · Price theft, fidelity and business interruption
All three are absent from both policies. Get them quoted so the school can decide rather than inherit the omission. Fidelity first.
7 · Glass, money and accidental damage
R10,000, R50,000 and R20,000 respectively. Confirm these are considered figures and not defaults, and re quote on the real numbers.
